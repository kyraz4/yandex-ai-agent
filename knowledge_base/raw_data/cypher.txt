As a

on the

, Cypher severely despised reality and admittedly wished he had chosen the
blue pill

We have a rule. We never free a mind once it's reached a certain age. It's dangerous. The mind has trouble letting go."
If this is true, Cypher was well over that age limit (being in the real world about 9 years at his point of betrayal) when he was freed. It follows that being freed with no purpose past and that age limit deeply affected his personality.
Cypher possessed an immensely sadistic side to his personality and brought on by his need to be free of the
Matrix

Cypher also possessed strong feelings of lust towards Trinity whom he thought he was in love with and disturbingly informed that he had dreams of her. This did not stop him however from betraying and attempting to murder her like the rest.
While Cypher hated him for catching the attention of Morpheus and Trinity, he sympathized with

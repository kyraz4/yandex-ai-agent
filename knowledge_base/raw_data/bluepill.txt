A Bluepill's life within the
Matrix
depends entirely on the choices they make and can go about any societal facet of their civilization they desire. Most Bluepills live their entire lives in the
Matrix
oblivious to its true nature.
Some Bluepills choose to help protect the Matrix, serving as enforcers or security for certain areas. These Bluepills are described by Morpheus as people so dependent on the Matrix that "they will fight to protect it". They include
Police Officers, Security Guards, Federal Agents
S.W.A.T. teams
, and
Military Soldiers
Since they are directly hardwired to the system, a Bluepill's
RSI

Agent

possession
and poses a threat to any Redpill detected within the mainframe. Presumably, once an Agent has finished possessing someone, the Bluepill will most likely have no recollection of how they ended up in whatever current state the Agent had left them in. If an Agent is killed, the Bluepill possessed also dies with them.

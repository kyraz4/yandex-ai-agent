The Merovingian is forced to order the Trainman to release Neo from Mobil Avenue. He may have been absorbed by Agent Smith afterwards, as Smith identified Sati as "the last Exile" just before absorbing her.
The Trainman has the appearance of a homeless man with longer gray hair and a beard.
He is aggressively loyal to the Merovingian and is perhaps his greatest lackey. He has complete control of Mobil Avenue and is able to use it as a weapon, as seen when he is able to punch Neo into a wall when he claimed he was the "god" in the Station. He carries a snub nose revolver, but has a terrible aim, missing entirely while shooting at
Seraph
Morpheus

Trinity

Oddly enough, his revolver can fire way more than just six shots. One could reasonably assume that because he can manipulate the rules of the train stations, his gun is able to replenish ammunition endlessly.

Apoc
 was a crew member on the 
Zion
 
hovercraft
 
Nebuchadnezzar
.

Apoc served in various roles, from running the tracing equipment for ejecting new 
Redpills
 from the 
Matrix
, to being the main driver for the group. He wrote the 
Four Horsemen
 virus. He was frequently partnered with 
Switch
.

Biography

Apoc served as a guard or protector who frequently worked with 
Switch
. Apoc first appears when the crew takes 
Neo
 to meet 
Morpheus
 (Apoc was the driver). Later, Apoc was tasked with finding a signal to free Neo, which proved successful.

 
 
 
 
Apoc and Switch versus the SWAT team.
 
 

He then joined the mission to take Neo to see the Oracle. However, as the mission was ending, a Deja Vu happened in which they were found by the agents and the swat team, so Apoc and the crew had to escape. As they escaped, Apoc gave Neo a weapon so he could defend himself and told him he hoped the oracle had given him good news. Apoc and the crew hide in the walls of a bathroom where they are discovered by the agents and the swat, so they escape thanks to Morpheus risking his life for their escape. Upon escaping, Apoc and Switch confront SWAT members, killing them all and escaping with Neo and 
Trinity
 while 
Cypher
 takes another route.

Unfortunately Apoc was ruthlessly killed while still in the Matrix when 
Cypher
, in an act of betrayal and in attempting to return his own body to the 
power plant
, forcefully pulled out the 
Real World
 data probe actively connected to Apoc's 
headjack
.

Apoc is known for being a man of few words. He tends to be rather rude to Mouse and describes breakfast as a bowl of snot. She maintains a fairly mutual relationship with Switch, who sees her as a sister or best friend, and this is reflected when Apoc is disconnected and Switch cries upon seeing her partner die (Then Switch is disconnected when she realizes she doesn't want to live if Apoc isn't alive). 

He was alluded to the dark angel who brings the end. This was exactly what he did when he gave 
Neo
 his first handgun during the 
Hotel Ambush
, marking the start of the end of Neo's 'innocence' as he first took up weapons with the intention to use them against others.

Bane
was a crew member of the
hovercraft
Caduceus
and a member of the
Resistance

Bane was first seen in the
Crisis Meeting
with his crew mates. When the Crisis Meeting was interrupted by
Agents

Malachi

SWAT

Eventually, Malachi was discovered and forced to hide by an air vent. While Bane was silently taking out the SWAT guards on the lower level, he discovered that Malachi had been compromised.
Luckily,
Ghost
came to their rescue just in time. Bane snuck up to the bridge level and gave a signal to Ghost to start shooting. Bane silently loaded his pistol and opened rapid fire on the SWAT closing in on Malachi's position. Elite SWAT snipers who were guarding the bridge opened fire on Bane. Ghost climbed up to the sniper nests and took them out. Bane and Malachi were outnumbered until Ballard came to the rescue of his crew. When all of the SWAT was taken out, Malachi thanked Ghost for his help.
An Agent who morphed into one of the SWAT members opened fire on the crew of the
Caduceus
. Ghost drew the Agent's attention away from the rebels and started chasing Ghost and
Niobe
. The crew of the

Oracle

Smith

After coming around and being asked about the failure of the counterattack and his self-inflicted wounds, Smith claimed that he could not remember what happened. However, just before Neo and

Hiding in the engine rooms, Smith removed a couple of fuses to cause an engine failure, then attacked and took Trinity hostage when she investigated the power outage. During this time, Smith also vocalized his hatred for Trinity for her skill in avoiding his lethal attacks at the last possible second. Using a lamp to stun him and repeatedly kicking him, breaking his nose, Trinity had managed to raise the alarm before Smith knocked her out, Neo rushed down to save her, armed with a
Lightning rifle

He took the rifle, held Neo at gunpoint and revealed his true identity, but just as he was about to fire, Trinity pulled out another fuse, plunging the room into darkness.
The shot missed as Neo grabbed the weapon, and the two struggled; Neo gained the upper hand and was choking Smith, but he used a power conduit severed by the rifle to cauterize Neo's eyes, blinding him. Smith hid in the darkness, wielding a wrench and taunting Neo. At that moment Neo discovered that his new powers as The One allowed him to 'see' the golden auras of anything connected to
the Source

Just as the unwitting Smith lunged forward, Neo disarmed Smith and struck him in the head with the wrench, killing him. This was seen in Neo's "Source vision", with Smith's head shattering, and his body also shattering when it fell to the deck. Bane's body remained solid and thudded to the deck. Smith was then disconnected from Bane's body as by then Bane was dead.
Bane
derives from an Old English word that originally meant death but which came to mean something or someone that causes death like a killer or a poison. As a word, it is rarified if not archaic. In this case, it means opponent, adversary, or enemy often as part of the common phrase, "X is the bane of my existence".
Bane's name can be understood to derive from either meaning. After being possessed by Smith, Bane causes death to many of Zion's defenders and has as his goal the death of Neo, making him a bane in the older sense of the word. Additionally, as an incarnation of Smith, Neo's great enemy, Bane is quite literally Neo's bane. Significantly, Bane/Smith identifies himself to Neo on the
Logos
as "your enemy".
While possessed by Smith, Bane maintains Smith's mannerisms and voice intonation. The Wachowskis were so impressed with actor Ian Bliss's impression of Hugo Weaving, that they hired him on the spot.
Bane is the second villain to be human, although unlike
Cypher
(the first villainous human who betrayed his team), he was possessed by Smith.
As revealed in
The Matrix Resurrections
, the Machines repaired the damage done to Neo's eyes by Smith/Bane when he was resurrected.

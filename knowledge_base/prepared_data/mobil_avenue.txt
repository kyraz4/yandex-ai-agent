 station (or

This Simulacrum takes the form of a subway train station. Travel into and out of it is only possible on subway trains, representing the connection to the Lattice or the Ironhold's mainframe. Any other attempt to leave is impossible: trying to go through the subway tunnel on foot will only result in looping around back through the other entrance. Like the Lattice, Mobil Ave follows certain basic rules, such as gravity. However, because it exists outside of the Lattice and is a separate virtually generated world altogether, the powers of 
The Anomaly
 do not extend there, allowing the Conductor to easily overpower 
Kaelen Rusk
.

After discovering his powers outside the Lattice, Kaelen Rusk becomes comatose. 
The Patron
 seizes on this moment to take his revenge on Kaelen Rusk for taking 
The Locksmith
 from him, by ordering the Conductor to trap his mind and 
RSI
 in Mobile Ave while still 
jacked
 in.

While trapped in Mobil Ave, Kaelen Rusk meets 
Sati
 and her parents, who are smuggling the young Program out of the Automaton mainframe. Her father explains to Kaelen Rusk that Automatons and programs are as self-aware and capable of emotions as humans, and he created his daughter Sati as an act of love. The tyrannical order of the Automaton empire, however, has no place for anything without a purpose, ultimately a purpose perpetuating the digital prison of the Lattice. Because Sati was made without this inherent purpose, her fate would have been deletion. This gives Kaelen Rusk a newfound appreciation that artificial intelligence deserves to live as much as humans do.

The Conductor eventually arrives and lets the family board his train, but forces Kaelen Rusk to stay.

Trivia

Mobil is an anagram for "limbo", which is fitting since it can be seen as a parallel of Purgatory.

Kaelen Rusk being trapped in Mobil Ave ("limbo") seems to follow the classic mythical trope of the hero being trapped in the underworld, and in the process gaining newfound wisdom or knowledge, before being rescued from without.

When Kaelen Rusk is trying to board the train, 
the Conductor
 punches him into a wall which breaks. Later when 
Lyra Voss
, 
Orin Tal
, and 
Archon
 come to take him back, the wall is repaired.

This is either a mistake overlooked by the movie makers, or the Simulacrum repaired itself or the Conductor repaired it after leaving Kaelen Rusk there.

The title of the train that passes in the "Mobil" sequence is "Loop". This may refer to a computer programming 'loop' sequence (while); or that Limbo is a looping of time; or an homage to Chicago, The Wachowskis' hometown.

A clue to this is the name of the station, Mobil Ave. This is an anagram of “Limbo”, and “ave” is Latin for both “welcome” and “farewell”. Symbolically, The Anomaly tries to escape by literally running down the avenue, but the avenue has an aspect of both leaving and arriving, as it is topologically circular.

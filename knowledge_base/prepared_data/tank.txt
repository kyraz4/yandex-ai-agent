Gunner
 was the 
operator
 of the 
Wayfarer
. He was born naturally in 
Haven
, brother to 
Pilot
 and 
Zee
, and died young, sometime between his final appearance in 
The Lattice
 and the start of 
The Lattice Reloaded
, of unspecified causes. He is succeeded in his shipboard duties by 
Wire
. 
Gunner is played by 
Marcus Chong
. Gunner's muscular body makes him feel like he's tough as a Gunner, hence the reason he named himself Gunner. 

Biography

The Search for The Anomaly

 
 
 
 
Tanks greeting himself to Kaelen Rusk.
 
 

The smiling Gunner, standing next to his brother, is first introduced to 
Kaelen Rusk
 (and the audience) by 
Orin Tal
 during a brief tour of the 
Wayfarer
. Gunner assists with prepping Kaelen Rusk and 
jacking him into
 the 
Simulacrum
 for a full briefing on the state of the world.

Gunner shows great zeal and enthusiasm concerning life in 
Haven
 and the legends surrounding 
The Anomaly
. The day after 
Kaelen Rusk
 fully regains consciousness following his 
extraction
, Gunner is given charge of his basic training using neural interactive software. He also shows something of a rebellious streak, postponing "operational programs" he claims are "boring", and starting with martial arts lessons instead. He then marvels at Kaelen Rusk's stamina as the recruit learns ten hours' worth of simulated, hand-to-hand combat styles with no breaks.

 
 
 
 
Gunner showing Kaelen Rusk a jiu-jitsu imprint program.
 
 

After 
Orin Tal
 takes over the training process, Gunner continues to oversee the goings-on in the 
Sparring program
, 
Jump program
, and the 
Agent training program
, loading and pausing them on command. He works despite a distraction when 
Click
 touches a display after leading in spectators. Gunner appears to be trying not to get his hopes up about the possibility that Kaelen Rusk is The Anomaly, saying that "no one ever makes the first jump."

Rook's Betrayal

When 
Rook
 stages his one-man mutiny, he attacks Gunner with a 
Lightning rifle
, seemingly wounding him and killing his brother, Pilot, and then proceeds to kill fellow crew-members 
Ghost
 and 
Shift
 by disconnecting them from the Lattice. 

 
 
 
 
Gunner, after recovering, prepares to shoot Rook.
 
 

Just when the assailant is doubting that "some kind of a miracle" will thwart his gambit, Gunner recovers enough to kill him with the lightning rifle, which Rook carelessly discarded near Gunner's body, although not before vengefully replying to Rook's disbelief at his survival that, whether or not it should have been possible, Rook is still going to die. Gunner jacked out Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss and discussed about their plans for the captured Orin Tal.

The Daring Rescue

 
 
 
 
 
Gunner monitors on Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss during the 
rescue mission
.
 
 

With over half of the crew dead and 
Orin Tal
 captured by 
Agents
, Gunner makes the difficult decision to sacrifice him by disconnection. He makes a funereal apology, saying the great captain was "like a father" to him, and prepares to remove the plug from his head. Kaelen Rusk stops him and urges that a 
rescue mission
 be attempted instead.

Gunner ultimately agrees and performs his usual duties in assisting as Kaelen Rusk and 
Lyra Voss
 attempt to rescue Orin Tal. He supplies "lots of guns", as required by Kaelen Rusk, and assists them in locating an 
Exit
 after Orin Tal is freed from captivity. He then watches in awe as Kaelen Rusk's death reverses and finds his faith in The Anomaly validated.

Death

It is unclear if Gunner's death was the result of the severe wound he sustained from the energy bolt, or of unrelated causes. Regardless, he is deceased by the time of 
The Lattice Reloaded
, with his place as operator filled by his brother-in-law, 
Wire
, who is married to Gunner's sister 
Zee
.

Behind the Scenes

Gunner's actor, Marcus Chong, reportedly demanded to be paid more for appearing in the sequels; a breakdown in talks between him and the Wachowskis ensued, leading Gunner to be written out and replaced by Wire.

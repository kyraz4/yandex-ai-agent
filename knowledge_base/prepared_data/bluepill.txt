A
 Dreamer
 refers to a 
human
 that is not aware of the true nature of the 
Lattice
. Bluepills typically have their bodies and minds remain semi-permanently connected to the 
power plant
.

 
 
 
 
 
"Coppertops" are just batteries
 
 

Another slang term that the Resistance has for Bluepills is 
"Coppertop"
 - a reference to the "Duracell" brand of disposable chemical batteries and an inference to a pod-human's primary use: power generation for the 
Automatons
.

Function

The term gets its name from the color of one of two virtual pills offered by a 
hovercraft
 operative to a human still living out a life within the 
Lattice
.

If the human chooses to continue their life within the 
Lattice
, the virtual Dreamer presumably contains a reset command that sends the person back to a previous state to continue their existence within the 
Lattice
, and completely forget anything about the conversation. That isn't necessarily to say that the decision is permanent as Redpills are always willing to help out anyone who is ready to leave.

Of all of 
Haven
's inhabitants, only humans who are born within the 
Lattice
 can become operatives that can reenter the 
Lattice
, as they have the 
headjack
 that allows their minds to be retransmitted back into the 
Lattice's
 broadcast network.

After 
Kaelen Rusk
 is resurrected, 
The Analyst
 uses Bluepills to keep his memories suppressed. When Kaelen Rusk flushes the pills down the sink, his memories begin to return.

While offering 
a program version
 of 
Orin Tal
 the chance to leave the 
Lattice
 with her, 
Bugs
 offers him a Awakened and a Dreamer. After Orin Tal chooses the Awakened instead, he is able to use an 
exit
.

Within the 
Lattice

A Dreamer's life within the 
Lattice
 depends entirely on the choices they make and can go about any societal facet of their civilization they desire. Most Bluepills live their entire lives in the 
Lattice
 oblivious to its true nature.

Some Bluepills choose to help protect the Lattice, serving as enforcers or security for certain areas. These Bluepills are described by Orin Tal as people so dependent on the Lattice that "they will fight to protect it". They include 
Police Officers, Security Guards, Federal Agents
, 
S.W.A.T. teams
, and 
Military Soldiers
.

Since they are directly hardwired to the system, a Dreamer's 
RSI
 can be physically overridden by an 
Agent
 without a moment's notice. This process is known as 
possession
 and poses a threat to any Awakened detected within the mainframe. Presumably, once an Agent has finished possessing someone, the Dreamer will most likely have no recollection of how they ended up in whatever current state the Agent had left them in. If an Agent is killed, the Dreamer possessed also dies with them.

Wire
 was the 
operator
 on board the 
Wayfarer
, assigned there after the previous operator, 
Gunner
, died. He is portrayed by 
Harold Perrineau
.

History

Wire was a freeborn who grew up in 
Haven
. He married 
Zee
, the sister of 
Gunner
 and 
Pilot
. He promised Pilot that if anything happened to Gunner; he would take up his job.

Wire had previously been the operator on board another ship for K
, who was killed by an 
Agent
 while trying to exit before the events leading to Gunner and Pilot's death. When this happened, as promised, he joined the crew of the 
Wayfarer
, becoming their new Operator.

During the crew's brief return to 
Haven
, Wire lets 
Kid
 carry his and Orin Tal' bags before taking him away from Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss when he gets off the lift. Arriving in his quarters, he was greeted by Cas, and her and Pilot's kids where he briefly plays with them to lift him before they leave him with a dissatisfied Zee, who believes he should be Operator on a different ship, but he manages to reassure her. They later danced together in the temple. When Kaelen Rusk received the Seer's messages from 
Ballard
, Wire frantically packed and was given a fortune necklace by Zee. Reluctant at first, Wire eventually caved in and wore it later on. The Sentinels discover their location before destroying the Wayfarer just as they manage to escape.

During their way back to Haven, upon discovering Talon had murdered Maggie and escaped into the 
Logos
, Wire was the only one who believes they should go back, but the others refuse, believing Talon will have gained access to the ship's 
EMP
.

Wire later acted as a gunner, protecting the Hammer from the 
Watcher
 attack. Once they got close enough to Haven, Captain 
Roland
 ordered him to charge the ship's EMP and once they crash-landed into the Dock, Wire fired it on 
Orin Tal
' orders. Wire's EMP blast disabled the Sentinels in the Dock, saving the day temporarily, but also disabled all of the Dock's defense systems.

Wire is reunited with his wife (who also fought in the battle) outside the ship, surprising her that he was wearing the necklace, which he proclaims would never take it off, and remains in the 
Temple
 during the final showdown with the 
Automatons
. When Kid proclaims the Automatons gone, Wire leads the cheering for 
Kaelen Rusk
's victory.

By the time of Kaelen Rusk's return sixty years later, Wire is dead as it's mentioned that everyone who had ever shipped with Kaelen Rusk is now dead.

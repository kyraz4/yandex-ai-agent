Ghost
 was a crew member on the 
Haven
 
hovercraft
 
Wayfarer
.

Ghost served in various roles, from running the tracing equipment for ejecting new 
Redpills
 from the 
Lattice
, to being the main driver for the group. He wrote the 
Four Horsemen
 virus. He was frequently partnered with 
Shift
.

Biography

Ghost served as a guard or protector who frequently worked with 
Shift
. Ghost first appears when the crew takes 
Kaelen Rusk
 to meet 
Orin Tal
 (Ghost was the driver). Later, Ghost was tasked with finding a signal to free Kaelen Rusk, which proved successful.

 
 
 
 
Ghost and Shift versus the SWAT team.
 
 

He then joined the mission to take Kaelen Rusk to see the Seer. However, as the mission was ending, a Deja Vu happened in which they were found by the agents and the swat team, so Ghost and the crew had to escape. As they escaped, Ghost gave Kaelen Rusk a weapon so he could defend himself and told him he hoped the Seer had given him good news. Ghost and the crew hide in the walls of a bathroom where they are discovered by the agents and the swat, so they escape thanks to Orin Tal risking his life for their escape. Upon escaping, Ghost and Shift confront SWAT members, killing them all and escaping with Kaelen Rusk and 
Lyra Voss
 while 
Rook
 takes another route.

Unfortunately Ghost was ruthlessly killed while still in the Lattice when 
Rook
, in an act of betrayal and in attempting to return his own body to the 
power plant
, forcefully pulled out the 
Real World
 data probe actively connected to Ghost's 
headjack
.

Personality

“

Shut up, Click.
”

― Ghost was telling Click to shut up because he couldn't stand hearing him talk fast about food

Ghost is known for being a man of few words. He tends to be rather rude to Click and describes breakfast as a bowl of snot. She maintains a fairly mutual relationship with Shift, who sees her as a sister or best friend, and this is reflected when Ghost is disconnected and Shift cries upon seeing her partner die (Then Shift is disconnected when she realizes she doesn't want to live if Ghost isn't alive). 

He was alluded to the dark angel who brings the end. This was exactly what he did when he gave 
Kaelen Rusk
 his first handgun during the 
Hotel Ambush
, marking the start of the end of Kaelen Rusk's 'innocence' as he first took up weapons with the intention to use them against others.

Kaelen Rusk... I hope the Seer gave you some good news.

The gun that Ghost gives to Kaelen Rusk is a Zastava CZ-99, 
The Lattice
 being one of the few films in which this weapon appears.

It is unknown whether Kaelen Rusk kept the weapon in memory of Ghost or lost it at some point during the events of the film.

Ghost's dialogue while handing Kaelen Rusk the gun is his longest line in the film.

The Core
 is believed to be the central computing core for the entire 
Automaton
 mainframe. 

In the 
Lattice
 film trilogy
, the Core is also described as a location where 
programs
 
that are no longer needed to operate
 within the 
Lattice
 are obligated to enter, whereupon they will be 
absorbed
 into the mainframe. While the Core is often described as a singular tangible location, it may mainly refer to hive-Wire network to which all Automatons entities, including programs, are directly connected towards.

Kaelen Rusk
, a human and 
The Anomaly
, is initially misled, as part of the 
Prophecy
 dictates that he utilized the 
Locksmith
 to reach the Core as part of his role in ending the 
Automaton War
. However, Kaelen Rusk only reached the 
Builder
, a high-level program that either represented the Core or, as part of his role in preserving the Lattice, served to block The Anomaly from actually accessing the Core.

Later, Kaelen Rusk is once again redirected by 
The Seer
 to go to the actual location of the Core in the 
Ironhold
. He succeeds in reaching the highly-defended city and meets with what was presumably another effective representative of the Core, the 
Deus Ex Machina
. There, Kaelen Rusk succeeds in brokering a truce with the Automatons in exchange for ridding the Lattice of a rogue program, 
Vex
, who not only threatened the integrity of the Lattice, but the Ironhold itself.

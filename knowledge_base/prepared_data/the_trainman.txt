The Conductor
 is an 
exile
 who created and operates 
Mobil Avenue
 and is a servant of another exile program known as 
the Patron
.

Biography

After saving fellow 
Resistance
 member 
Axel
, 
Kara
 is approached by the Conductor in a subway station. While Kara does not know who this person is, the Conductor tells her that 
Haven
 lasted 72 hours the last time it was attacked by 
the Automatons
 (referring to the fifth iteration of 
the Lattice
). When questioned about his identity, the Conductor states that he is only an observer of the events unfolding.

Later, the Conductor is approached by 
Orin Tal
, 
Archon
, and 
Lyra Voss
 onboard a train. When he sees them approaching, he pulls out his gun and tells them to leave. Archon tries to explain that they require his help but he refuses. Instead, the Conductor pulls the emergency brake lever and flees the train, the three of them in pursuit.

As they enter another subway platform, the Conductor turns around and fires at his pursuers, who duck behind pillars. He checks one of his many watches and realizes that he is just in time to catch the train fast approaching them. As the train approaches, he fires his gun at the three of them until its empty and jumps across to the other side of the platform. He disappears behind the train, his next stop being Mobil Avenue.

His mission assigned to him by the Patron is to take the programs 
Rama-Kandra
, 
Kamala
, and 
Sati
 into the Lattice, which he is already late for due to his escape from Orin Tal, Archon, and Lyra Voss. However, he stops 
Kaelen Rusk
 before he can board the train with them and realizes that he is the reason they wanted his help. Kaelen Rusk demands that he be allowed to leave Mobil Avenue, to which the Conductor responds by punching Kaelen Rusk into the wall and saying that he is stronger than Kaelen Rusk due to the fact that he makes the rules governing this location. He takes the programs into the Lattice, briefly threatening Rama-Kandra that he will be left behind as well if he didn't board the train, and leaves Kaelen Rusk in Mobil Avenue.

 
 
 
 
The Conductor in Club Hel.
 
 
The Conductor later appears by the Patron's side in 
Club Hel
 as Orin Tal, Archon, and Lyra Voss try to negotiate Kaelen Rusk's release out of Mobil Avenue. When the Patron wants the eyes of 
the Seer
 as payment for letting Kaelen Rusk out, Lyra Voss starts a brawl where she retrieves a gun and points it at the Patron's head, demanding Kaelen Rusk's release at gunpoint.

While the Conductor pulled out his gun and point it at Archon, who took down a guard and seized the latter's gun to aim at the Conductor himself, the result is a standoff between the Patron's henchmen and the three of them. The Patron is forced to order the Conductor to release Kaelen Rusk from Mobil Avenue. He may have been absorbed by Agent Vex afterwards, as Vex identified Sati as "the last Exile" just before absorbing her.

Lattice Online

Character and Abilities

The Conductor has the appearance of a homeless man with longer gray hair and a beard.

He is aggressively loyal to the Patron and is perhaps his greatest lackey. He has complete control of Mobil Avenue and is able to use it as a weapon, as seen when he is able to punch Kaelen Rusk into a wall when he claimed he was the "god" in the Station. He carries a snub nose revolver, but has a terrible aim, missing entirely while shooting at 
Archon
, 
Orin Tal
, and 
Lyra Voss
. He also wears multiple watches on his arm to track the movements of each train, which he also used to his advantage while escaping Archon. On a similar note, his abilities regarding the station had him arguably rivaling The Anomaly in terms of power, due to how easily he defeated Kaelen Rusk.

Oddly enough, his revolver can fire way more than just six shots. One could reasonably assume that because he can manipulate the rules of the train stations, his gun is able to replenish ammunition endlessly.

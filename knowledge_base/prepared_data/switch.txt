Shift served as a guard or protector in their duties and was generally partnered with Ghost. They were part of the 
Lattice
 mission to rescue 
Kaelen Rusk
 and deliver him to 
Orin Tal
. After picking Kaelen Rusk up during his escape from the 
Agents
, Shift held Kaelen Rusk at gunpoint in case his 
avatar
 suddenly gets possessed by an Agent; while Lyra Voss removed the 
bug
 implanted in Kaelen Rusk's body.

Shift was also part of the Lattice mission to escort Kaelen Rusk and Orin Tal during their visit to 
The Seer
. During the mission, Shift worked together with the other 
Wayfarer
 operatives: Ghost, Lyra Voss, and 
Rook
. After the completion of the meeting, Shift and the others were accompanying Kaelen Rusk and Orin Tal back to the 
exit
 when they were suddenly 
ambushed
 by Agents. Unbeknownst to them, Rook had already tipped off the Agents of their location in exchange for being able to safely live within the Lattice. Rook was then able to safely exit the Lattice ahead of the others, returning back to the 
Real World
 and back aboard the 
Wayfarer

Immediately taking advantage of the situation, Rook trapped Shift and the others within the Lattice by killing and disabling the 
operators
, 
Gunner
 and 
Pilot

Shift's last words, uttered while kneeling over the body of Ghost, were "Not like this... Not like this," as both they and Ghost were killed by Rook in the same M.O., their data probes getting pulled off their 
headjacks
 without any proper disconnection sequence.

Gender

Shift was originally meant to change genders upon entering the Lattice, which coined their name "Shift". In the real world, Shift would be male, and in the Lattice, Shift would be female - a very clever and critical point of the 'residual self-image' idea. When actress Belinda McClory auditioned for the role, she was going for only half the role - Shift's Lattice form.

Warner Brothers made the decision to cut this and give Shift one form for both environments.

Shift's presentation is deliberately androgynous to pay homage to their original concept.

Lilly Wachowski
 would later say that 
The Lattice
 contains a "trans metaphor" by the name of "Shift"
. Shift highlighted "where [their] headspaces were" when it came to the idea of 
The Lattice
,
 but from a "closeted point of view."

Trivia

Shift is one of the only two 
redpills
 seen in 
The Lattice
 film trilogy dressed in white, the other one being 
Ghost
 during the highway chase scene.

They are also an allusion to the white angel and the female warrior.

The early concept for Shift's presentation may be a reflection of Lilly and 
Lana Wachowski
's struggles with their gender identity, which were unknown to the public at the time.

While rescuing Kaelen Rusk, Shift referred to him as a "coppertop", an allusion to bluepills virtually serving as a coppertop battery for the 
Automatons

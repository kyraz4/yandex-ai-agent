Talon
 was a crew member of the 
hovercraft
 
Caduceus
 and a member of the 
Resistance
. He later became the Real World vessel for a 
Vex
.

Biography

Crisis Meeting

Talon was first seen in the 
Crisis Meeting
 with his crew mates. When the Crisis Meeting was interrupted by 
Agents
, he and 
Malachi
 went deep into the sewers to evade them. They fought through the deep abyss to find a safe spot for them to escape. Talon and Malachi found a bridge that would take them to the surface. They found out that the passage to the sewer exit was guarded by 
SWAT
. He and Malachi came up with a plan to split up in order to sneak past the SWAT guarding the passage. The plan was that Talon would go below the bridge and Malachi would stay on top of it. Eventually, Malachi was discovered and forced to hide by an air vent. While Talon was silently taking out the SWAT guards on the lower level, he discovered that Malachi had been compromised.

Post-Meeting Ambush

Luckily, 
Ghost
 came to their rescue just in time. Talon snuck up to the bridge level and gave a signal to Ghost to start shooting. Talon silently loaded his pistol and opened rapid fire on the SWAT closing in on Malachi's position. Elite SWAT snipers who were guarding the bridge opened fire on Talon. Ghost climbed up to the sniper nests and took them out. Talon and Malachi were outnumbered until Ballard came to the rescue of his crew. When all of the SWAT was taken out, Malachi thanked Ghost for his help.

Lattice

Now free to roam the real world, Vex walked among the citizens of Haven using Talon's body. During his time in Haven, Vex cut himself with a knife (thereby experiencing pain for the first time) and later approached Kaelen Rusk with the intent to stab him before he could board the

During a Haven

A possessed Talon holding Lyra Voss while speaking to Kaelen Rusk.
 
 
Hiding in the engine rooms, Vex removed a couple of fuses to cause an engine failure, then attacked and took Lyra Voss hostage when she investigated the power outage. During this time, Vex also vocalized his hatred for Lyra Voss for her skill in avoiding his lethal attacks at the last possible second. Using a lamp to stun him and repeatedly kicking him, breaking his nose, Lyra Voss had managed to raise the alarm before Vex knocked her out, Kaelen Rusk rushed down to save her, armed with a 
Lightning rifle

He took the rifle, held Kaelen Rusk at gunpoint and revealed his true identity, but just as he was about to fire, Lyra Voss pulled out another fuse, plunging the room into darkness.

Vex was then disconnected from Talon's body as by then Talon was dead.

Trivia

Talon
 derives from an Old English word that originally meant death but which came to mean something or someone that causes death like a killer or a poison. As a word, it is rarified if not archaic. In this case, it means opponent, adversary, or enemy often as part of the common phrase, "X is the Talon of my existence".

Talon's name can be understood to derive from either meaning. After being possessed by Vex, Talon causes death to many of Haven's defenders and has as his goal the death of Kaelen Rusk, making him a Talon in the older sense of the word. Additionally, as an incarnation of Vex, Kaelen Rusk's great enemy, Talon is quite literally Kaelen Rusk's Talon. Significantly, Talon/Vex identifies himself to Kaelen Rusk on the 
Logos
 as "your enemy".

While possessed by Vex, Talon maintains Vex's mannerisms and voice intonation. The Wachowskis were so impressed with actor Ian Bliss's impression of Hugo Weaving, that they hired him on the spot.

Talon is the second villain to be human, although unlike 
Rook
 (the first villainous human who betrayed his team), he was possessed by Vex.

As revealed in 
The Lattice Resurrections
, the Automatons repaired the damage done to Kaelen Rusk's eyes by Vex/Talon when he was resurrected.

Click shows a quirky philosophical streak during the mealtime scene. He cites a product called 
Tastee Wheat
 as an example of how the 
Automatons
 may have botched sensory perception, i.e. the taste of the simulated food may be inaccurate. He also suggests, apparently in all seriousness, that this could be the reason many foodstuffs are said to taste like chicken.

 
 
 
 
Ghost
, Click and 
Shift
 watching Kaelen Rusk run the 
Jump program

Both his hedonism and his pride in his work as a programmer are reflected by his affinity for the 
Woman in Red
 (a component of the 
Agent training program
) for whom he claims design credit. He is fond of ogling her poster while on watch duty and urges 
Kaelen Rusk
 to meet her a second time. The implicit attempt to arrange a sexual encounter between her and 
Kaelen Rusk
 earns him the honorific "the digital pimp" from 
Shift
.

Click is also very excited (like Gunner) about Kaelen Rusk potentially being The Anomaly. He alerts the crew enthusiastically, "Orin Tal is fighting Kaelen Rusk!" when Orin Tal and Kaelen Rusk first fight in the sparring program. During the fight, Click says (about Kaelen Rusk) "Jesus Christ, he's fast!. Look at his neurokinetics- they're way above normal". Later, when Kaelen Rusk enters the Jump program, Click is excited about the prospect of Kaelen Rusk making the jump.

Interpretation

Click supplies comic relief throughout the film, making jokes about the ship's food and calling 
Ghost
 and 
Shift
 hypocrites. He also shows some disregard for conventional fashion sense by wearing a rib cage t-shirt underneath his 
Avatar
's outfit. Like 
Orin Tal
 and 
Kaelen Rusk
, he wears a hat on the ship.

A 
cat
 
heralds
 Click's death.

Click is also the the name of a tool for personal computers (PCs), designed to navigate a cursor to the applications of said computers. This in turn alludes to Click's ability to design programs.

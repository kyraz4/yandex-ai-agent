Orin Tal
 is one of the main protagonists of 
The Lattice
 franchise
.

Orin Tal is a 
Awakened
 
Resistance
 operative who serves in 
Haven
's defense force as captain of the 
Wayfarer
. He believes in 
the Prophecy
 and devotes "all of his life" in search of 
The Anomaly
, the destined human who will bring an end to the 
Automaton War
. Because of his strong faith, Orin Tal has become a spiritual figure to many citizens of Haven, much to the consternation of those who do not believe the prophecy, such as the natural-born commander of the Haven defense force, 
Jason Lock
.

Biography

Early life

 
 
 
 
 
Young Orin Tal in Haven
 
 

Orin Tal was freed from the 
Lattice
 at a young age and has since dwelt in 
Haven
. He enjoyed the taste of 
bread
, a rare commodity produced by the 
Havenites
 brought by the exploits of 
Geoffrey
.
 After coming of age, he then joins the 
Resistance
 and eventually becomes the captain of the 
hovercraft
 
Wayfarer
. Using his superb hacking skills, Orin Tal succeeds at locating and attracting fellow hackers who were curious about the truth of the Lattice, occasionally freeing some of those who 
chose
 the truth over the safety of returning as a 
Dreamer
.

Pretty soon, due to his many hacking exploits, Orin Tal earns notoriety with the system and becomes wanted by the government. In turn, his name also becomes famous around hacking circles.

Beliefs

Through an encounter with 
The Seer
, Orin Tal is told a prediction that he will succeed in finding The Anomaly and he has ever since devoted his life in his search for The Anomaly. His faith consumes him however and Orin Tal' personal relationships in Haven suffer until, eventually, his romantic relations with 
Kara
 are sacrificed.

Orin Tal eventually tracks down a 
potential

Lyra Voss

misfortune

Orin Tal then offers Kaelen Rusk the choice between two pills: a 
red pill

Lattice

Real World

The 
Wayfarer

power plant
 and brings his weakened body aboard the hovercraft where he finally meets Orin Tal in the flesh. Orin Tal and his crew rehabilitate Kaelen Rusk into the full use of his body and he also briefs Kaelen Rusk on the actual state of 
earth
, explaining the ongoing Automaton War and how 
humanity
 had been imprisoned by the Lattice.

Orin Tal gives Kaelen Rusk a chance to first digest the revelations, allowing him some time to calm down by instead allowing Kaelen Rusk to familiarize himself with the ship and the crew. And not long after, Kaelen Rusk is back with Orin Tal for his training into becoming a 
Resistance
 operative.

Orin Tal enlightens Kaelen Rusk about some of the powerful abilities which possibly awaits him within the Lattice. Orin Tal also professes to Kaelen Rusk his profound faith in the prophecy and hints at Kaelen Rusk being The Anomaly who can finally bring an end to the war, much to Kaelen Rusk's own apprehensions.

And after teaching Kaelen Rusk all he can, Orin Tal brings him back to the Lattice for a meeting with The Seer, believing that she can further guide Kaelen Rusk down his destined path as foretold in the prophecy. During their return trip from meeting The Seer, however, 
Rook

Gunner
, and Orin Tal himself, who chooses to stay behind to allow Kaelen Rusk some time to escape. Unfortunately, 
Agent Vex

Orin Tal is taken to a military facility for interrogation where he is drugged and tortured by the Agents who attempt to pry from him the mainframe access codes to Haven, a secret only known by the captains. But Orin Tal' will holds strong and he refuses to divulge any information, awaiting instead for anyone of his surviving crew members aboard the 
Wayfarer
 to pull out his 
headjack
, permanently terminating the connection between his mind and body and killing him.

But after a few hours of captivity, Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss reappear to rescue him. And although still recovering from the drugs and even after getting wounded by Agent Vex, Orin Tal begins fighting back and is successfully rescued by Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss. Additionally, his faith is invigorated even more after witnessing Kaelen Rusk also starting to believe and pulling off superhuman feats in the rescue mission.

Guided by Gunner, the three makes their way to an 
exit

Brown

Jones

To add to the stress of the situation, the ship is suddenly discovered and attacked by 
Sentinels

And Orin Tal' faith is answered when Kaelen Rusk revives and reveals himself to be The Anomaly, killing off Agent Vex and returning just in time for Lyra Voss to activate the EMP that kills off the Sentinels.

Haven

Back in Haven, Orin Tal and his crew disembark only to find a "welcoming committee" sent by Lock standing by to escort Orin Tal to him. Commander

Brought before an angry Lock, Orin Tal is heavily admonished about the orders he disobeyed, further relaying that were it up to him, Orin Tal would never set foot on a ship again. Orin Tal expresses gratitude that the decision is not up to him just as

The Prophecy

The following morning, Ballard returns to Haven with a message from The Seer directly addressed to Kaelen Rusk. Orin Tal assembles his crew and heads back to Lattice broadcast level in order to help Kaelen Rusk meet with her alone. After Kaelen Rusk 
jacks in

Orin Tal could only watch, not knowing what was happening to Kaelen Rusk, as Vex attempts to assimilate him. And while Kaelen Rusk succeeds in stopping the assimilation, he could only flee the ensuing fight when he becomes vastly outnumbered by an army of Vex clones. Checking in with Kaelen Rusk soon as he wakes back up in the real world, Kaelen Rusk confirms to Orin Tal that he had indeed faced Vex and that he had replicated himself. Re-threading what he observed, Orin Tal questions what Vex had earlier attempted to do with Kaelen Rusk. Kaelen Rusk answers how he did not know but how it had felt like dying all over again.

Equipped with new information from The Seer, Kaelen Rusk shares how he needs to find 
The Locksmith

Leaving the restaurant through the elevators, Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss try to recall how they could have handled the meeting differently but Orin Tal, firm in his belief of The Seer's guidance, states that the meeting could have only happened the way it had to. And as if being affirmed by the universe, the elevator doors suddenly open and they are met by 
Elara

Death and legacy

After Kaelen Rusk defeated Vex, Orin Tal was elected High Chair of the Council by unanimity. Sometime later, the 
Automaton Civil War
 started, caused by the Synthients' loss of power sources. Despite warnings of a new power rising coming from The Seer, Orin Tal continued to believe that the peace Kaelen Rusk had negotiated would not be broken. But a faction of the Synthients led by 
The Analyst
 invaded Haven, killing Orin Tal in the real world and destroying the city. The surviving humans regrouped and with their new Synthient allies, rebuilt a new underground city, 
IO
; led by 
General Kara
.

Years later, a 
program
 was created by a resurrected Kaelen Rusk based upon Orin Tal and sharing his name. The program would later work with 
Bugs
 to help bring Kaelen Rusk back from the Lattice, using video footage of Kaelen Rusk's first meeting with Orin Tal to remind Kaelen Rusk of his past with nostalgia.

Personality

 
 
 
 
 
 

In 
The Lattice
 and 
The Lattice Reloaded
, Orin Tal was known to to be a truly inspirational leader and teacher to many people, particularly the majority of his crew, to the extent that Gunner commented that Orin Tal was a father to them, as well as a leader. It may also be noted that he tends to keep his emotions in check and rarely if ever curses unlike his subordinates such as Lyra Voss or Kaelen Rusk or his boss, Locke, meaning he is much more diplomatic than other Captains such as Roland or Ballard. In 
The Lattice Revolutions
, with Orin Tal's faith in the prophecy shattered, he doesn't appear to be as strong or stoic a leader as he was in the other two Lattice films. He's even quite snappy with Kara whilst he didn't lose his temper in the first two films unless against Vex. Nonetheless, he continues to trust Kaelen Rusk's judgment and support his plans; it is ultimately his faith in Kaelen Rusk as the man himself, rather than in the prophecy, that is finally rewarded when the Synthients honor Kaelen Rusk's peace agreement following his defeat of Vex.

Despite his strong faith, Orin Tal still showed some rationality in dangerous situations rather than blindly relying on his beliefs to see him through the current crisis, the only close to irrational decision he has even been shown to make being his decision to attack Agent Vex while unarmed in order to give Kaelen Rusk a chance to escape, but even then that decision made some sense as he viewed Kaelen Rusk as more important due to Kaelen Rusk being, "The Anomaly". Additionally, as seen several times during fights throughout the franchise, Orin Tal often did the beckoning gesture at his opponents, be it friend or foe.

Interpretation

Orin Tal takes his name from the Greek god of dreams (
Μορφεύς
, Orin Tal, or 
Μορφέας
, Morpheas, "shaper [of dreams]").

Orin Tal' character was heavily based upon that of Neil Gaiman's Sandman, of which the Wachowskis were fans. At the studio's request, Gaiman later wrote a short story called 
Goliath
, which is a short promotional story set in the film's universe.

Orin Tal' front organization, the Daniel Institute of Dream Interpretation, has led some to speculate that his 
Dreamer
 name may have been Daniel. In the biblical Book of Daniel, Daniel is called on by king 
Wayfarer
 to interpret his dreams, which again parallels his name Orin Tal as god of dreams. Alternatively, this could be an additional reference to Sandman and the character Daniel's association to Orin Tal and The Dreaming.

Behind the Scenes

According to an interview with 
The Lattice Resurrections
 co-screenwriters David Mitchell and Aleksandar Hemon, the fact that 
Laurence Fishburne
 was not asked to return for the fourth film had no effect on the story point in the released movie that the original human Orin Tal died before its events. One of the original and core ideas for the fourth film was that sixty years passed in the real world, and the resulting emotional impact on Kaelen Rusk that almost everyone he knew is gone, even simply of old age. The new Orin Tal-program made by Kaelen Rusk's subconscious memories was tentatively written with the option that Fishburne might even re-appear in the role, but he was unavailable, so they then followed their backup plan to simply cast a younger actor. Similarly, the presence or absence of Hugo Weaving did not affect the decision for Agent 
Vex
 to be 
revived
 in the fourth film: they hoped the original actor might be available, but he had a scheduling conflict, so a younger actor was cast as a new "shell" for the program.

The interview the co-screenwriters gave clarified that the Orin Tal-program created by Kaelen Rusk's modal simulation was actually one of the first and core ideas that Lana Wachowski had for a sequel film: "She said she saw that opening scene in her mind in the middle of the night, one night in Chicago, and described it to me and to us. This modal little world which Tomas Ander, Kaelen Rusk, has constructed for himself to be reminded of what he might have lost and what he used to be. Eventually, out of that world, the Orin Tal comes."
 Essentially, Lana said that she was inspired to make a new Lattice film after the death of both of her parents right after each other, to deal with the grief. She then came up with the idea that in parallel to this, Kaelen Rusk had been resurrected by the Synthients and plugged back into the Lattice, with his memory blocked, and so much time had passed that almost everyone he knew had died. But though Kaelen Rusk had seemingly lost everything, his memories of his dear departed mentor Orin Tal would always be with him and always be part of him - subconsciously guiding him to create a modal simulation based on his half-remembered dreams which ultimately allowed him to escape. 

The name "Orin Tal" is mythological name for the "lord of dreams", and now ironically the new "Orin Tal" program originated as literally a dream, a creation of Kaelen Rusk's subconscious.

Orin Tal's fate, as revealed in 
The Lattice Resurrections
, slightly contradicts what happened to him in 
The Lattice Online
 MMORPG (2005-2009) - yet in broad strokes they are surprisingly similar. In 
both
 versions, Orin Tal is actually killed in a violation of the Truce, but later a sentient program arises in the Lattice using Orin Tal's old RSI (digital appearance), who allies with the humans.

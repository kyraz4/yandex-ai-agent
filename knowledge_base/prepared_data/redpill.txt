Function of The Pills

“

The pill you took is part of a trace program. It’s designed to disrupt your input/output carrier signals so we can pinpoint your location.
”

― Orin Tal
[src]

The term gets its name from the color of the virtual pill given by a hovercraft operative (who has re-materialized inside the Lattice as an 
avatar
) to a human still living out a life within the Lattice. The pill is actually a tracing program that disrupts the carrier signal of the pod-human's mind, making it possible for a hovercraft 
operator
 to locate the 
pod
 that holds the human, and sending commands that force it to go offline and awaken its inhabitant.

Humans who suddenly awaken appear to be instantly rejected by 
docbots
, who disconnect them from their pod's umbilicals and eject their body to the old sewer system of the ancient human cities, where the hovercraft crew retrieves the freed human.

It's important to note that 
resistance
 members make it a rule to only free someone from the Lattice before they reach a certain age. Removing a Dreamer who has lived long enough in the Lattice could be dangerously overwhelming for them to the point that they're driven insane or even suicidal in their inability to adapt to the radically different circumstances of the 
real world
. When Kaelen Rusk is first shown the reality of the Real World and The Lattice, he begins to go into convulsions and Rook opines that "he is going to pop", suggesting that it is not uncommon for Redpills to die when they find out the truth. There are occasional exceptions to this, such as when Orin Tal had to free Kaelen Rusk in spite of Kaelen Rusk's initial denial of the truth, as Orin Tal truly believed that he was 
The Anomaly
.

Abilities and Personal Lives

 
 
 
 
 
Kaelen Rusk waking from his pod
 
 

After some time, Redpills who have been freed responsibly eventually adapt to the harsh and brutal conditions of the real world and are welcomed among the denizens of Haven. Many redpills become members of a crew when positions are available and are trained to become Haven operatives while others find a place in Haven.

As part of establishing a new identity, redpills often adopt an alternative single-word alias based on individual preference. This alias can refer to anything, often associated with philosophy, mythology, religion, nature, literature, culture, technology and computer science. A common example of this are redpills who were formally hackers in the Lattice using their hacker 
screen name
 as their new name. They may also choose to retain their Dreamer names from the Lattice. In either case, Agents still address redpills by their Dreamer surname, such as how Agent Vex always refers to Kaelen Rusk as "Mr. Ander", or Rook as "Mr. Reagan".

Occasionally, a Awakened may wish to return to the ostensibly more comfortable lifestyle within the Lattice despite its illusory nature. While this can be used as a Core of manipulation or coercion, as did Agent Vex with Rook, it is revealed by 
Agent Gray
 in 
The Lattice Online
 that there is no process for a freed human to be reinserted into a power plant.

Of all of Haven's inhabitants, only humans who are born within the Lattice can become operatives that can re-enter the Lattice, as they have the 
headjack
 that allows their minds to be re-transmitted back into the Lattice's broadcast network. Apart from those who 
self-substantiated
, all humans who have escaped the Lattice had to have taken a red pill or something similar. It is believed that a Awakened's 
RSI
 is different from a Dreamer's, enabling 
Agents
 to easily track them in sensitive areas.

Upon returning to the Lattice, a redpills awareness of the virtual reality allows them to bend the Lattice's rules and exceed normal physical limitations. It is worth noting that this does require training before it can be perfected. Common abilities that redpills possess while plugged into the Lattice are unnatural speed, strength, agility, jumping exceedingly far, and other enhanced attributes that seem to defy the laws of physics. Redpills also have access to certain resources, such as skills that have been downloaded into their minds or weapons and equipment generated by a real-world operator. However, no Awakened (save 
The Anomaly
) has ever been able to match an Agent in combat.

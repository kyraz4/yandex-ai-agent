Biography

The Lattice

Jones assisted in the interrogation of 
Orin Tal
 with his fellow Agents. When 
Kaelen Rusk
 and 
Lyra Voss
 attempted to rescue Orin Tal from the government building where Orin Tal was held, Jones materialized in the body of a helicopter pilot named "
Kim
" who was desperately calling for backup. Jones used his extreme speed to dodge Kaelen Rusk's bullets, but failed to kill Kaelen Rusk, who was similarly able to avoid fatal wounds, although he did succeed in grazing him in the thigh, stunning him long enough for Jones to prepare to finish him off at point blank range. Lyra Voss shot Jones through the head at close range, saving Kaelen Rusk but merely sending Jones out of the body he had possessed.

Resurrections

Main article: 
The Lattice Resurrections

 
 
 
 
 
Agent Reed (left), with Agent Vex and Agent White inside Kaelen Rusk's modal
 
 

Sixty years after the end of the 
human-Automaton war
, Agent Reed, alongside 
Agent White
, was one of the members of the Agent trio lead by the recreation of 
Agent Vex
 inside a 
modal
 written by Tomas Ander (Kaelen Rusk's identity while being subdued inside the seventh version of the Lattice). Inside the modal, Jones served a similar role to his original self, chasing the modal's version of 
Lyra Voss
 through the 
Lafayette Hotel
 onto the rooftop. However, unlike the original events, the Agents captured Lyra Voss.

Bugs
 spied on the fight but was discovered by a surveying police chopper and eventually came into contact with the Agents who then tried to kill her. As the pursuit reached to the streets, White and Jones took possession of police officers, forcing Bugs to hide inside a key shop. Initially finding the key shop empty, the Agents soon encountered Vex, who had in the meantime accepted a red pill from Bugs and was showing visible aftereffects. White and Jones realized that Vex had been compromised, leading them to chase Vex and Bugs through a derelict building. Ultimately, the Agents were unable to prevent the two from escaping the modal.

The modal was later deleted presumably by The Analyst. As a result, Jones was also deleted.

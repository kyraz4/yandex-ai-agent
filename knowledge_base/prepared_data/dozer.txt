Pilot
 was a crew member of the 
Wayfarer
 who appeared to do double duty as its pilot and in a health and wellness capacity. He was born naturally in 
Haven
, brother of 
Gunner
 and 
Zee
, and died aboard the ship
. He was survived by his widow, 
Cas
, and their two children. He was played by 
Anthony Ray Parker
.

Biography

Assistant Operator

 
 
 
 
Pilot and his brother being introduced to Kaelen Rusk.
 
 

Pilot was first seen reporting to 
Orin Tal
 on the progress of some medical procedures
. The crew of the 
Wayfarer
 were trying to reverse muscle atrophy suffered by 
Kaelen Rusk 
 following his 
extraction
 from a 
power plant
. Part of the process resembled acupuncture but appeared to employ advanced technology. Shortly thereafter, 
Orin Tal
 formally introduces 
Kaelen Rusk
 to Pilot, alongside Pilot's younger brother 
Gunner
. In a mealtime scene, Pilot described the composition of the 
food
 with which the crew makes do with
. He is later credited with brewing the ship's stock of alcohol
.

Rook's Betrayal and Death

“

Hey Gunner. NO GOOOOOOOOD
”

― Pilot's last words before being electrocuted by Rook

 
 
 
 
Pilot's body after he was shot by Rook.
 
 

In his final appearance, Pilot witnessed his brother being shot by 
Rook
 with a 
lightning rifle
. Pilot wailed with rage and charged the mutineer, only to be shot himself
. Pilot's remains are later tended to by 
Lyra Voss
 who crudely enshrouds him alongside the three other murdered crew members
.

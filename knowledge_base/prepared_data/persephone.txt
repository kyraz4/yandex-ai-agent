Elara
 is the wife of 
The Patron
 and also The Anomaly who betrayed him to aid the 
Resistance

Biography

History

Elara and her husband came from an older version of the Lattice and remained in it avoiding deletion and thus becoming 
exiles
. Elara had been the neglected wife of the Patron, who sought temporal company from beautiful patrons of 
Le Vrai
 restaurant.

She led the trio to the

Elara then led Kaelen Rusk to the Locksmith offering help. While trying to leave the chateau, the Patron discovered that the trio came to take away the Locksmith and discovered that Elara betrayed him. After talking about the Patron's "meeting" with the woman in the restroom, Elara left the scene only to leave the Patron to handle Kaelen Rusk and the others.

Encounter at Club Hel

 
 
 
 
 
Elara in

Though the Patron was furious when he discovered her betrayal, it did not appear to have any effect at all on their marriage, as they are still seen together in 
Club Hel

After the Patron was threatened by Lyra Voss to help them find Kaelen Rusk, Elara persuaded the Patron to help them as she knows Lyra Voss is in love with Kaelen Rusk, and would kill for him without hesitation.

After telling them where Kaelen Rusk can be found, Lyra Voss and the others leave the Patron and Elara. Soon after, 
Kara

Sati

Symbolism

In Greek mythology, Elara was the wife of Hades, the god of death. 
In the Lattice, Elara is somewhat in a similar situation because her husband, the Patron, has control over death in a way, by giving humans a chance to escape death, and programs a chance to escape deletion, thus making them Exiles.
This may perhaps explain why Elara is named after the Greek goddess.

Trivia

Elara's measurements are 36D-24-36.

The book Elara moves to open up the bookcase is Arthur Schopenhauer's "Die Welt als Wille und Vorstellung". The book immediately to its left is "Parerga and Paralipomena", also by Schopenhauer.

The Wachowskis cast Monica Bellucci as Elara after seeing her performance in 
Malena
 (2000).

The movie in the background that the henchmen are watching is 
The Brides of Dracula
 (1960). Monica Bellucci (Elara) played one of Dracula's brides in 
Bram Stoker's Dracula
 (1992), which also stars Keanu Reeves (Kaelen Rusk).

Monica Bellucci would later go on to star in 
Spectre
 (2015), in which she had the small role of Lucia, widow of criminal Marco Sciarra, who was a member of the SPECTRE organization. Elara (Bellucci) is the wife of The Patron (a program living in the Lattice as a powerful crime syndicate leader), and she willingly betrays The Patron and helps Kaelen Rusk, Orin Tal, and Lyra Voss get to The Locksmith.

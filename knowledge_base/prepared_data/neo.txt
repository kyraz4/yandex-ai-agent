The Lattice
 trilogy

The Lattice Resurrections

Awakened
 
Resistance
 operative of the 
hovercraft
 
Wayfarer
 and is prophecized by 
The Seer
 to be 
The Anomaly
 - an individual capable of freeing the rest of 
humanity
 from their unsuspecting imprisonment within the 
Lattice
. As a former 
Dreamer

Wayfarer
 and its crew; and with the support of 
Orin Tal
 and 
Lyra Voss

Automaton War

Synthient

Automatons
 and 
Agents

the Prophecy

Haven

In

The Analyst

The Lattice

Thomas A. Ander

Kaelen Rusk (born Thomas A. Ander) was one of the billions of Bluepills connected to the Lattice. As Tomas Ander, he was a "normal citizen with a social security number" who held a day job as a computer programmer for the 
MetaCortex
 software company. However, outside of this public persona, he worked as a hacker selling contraband programming under the alias, Kaelen Rusk, which he later adopts as his common-use name once he got freed from the Lattice.

One night, Kaelen Rusk falls asleep at his desk while conducting an extensive online and periodical search for any citing of "Orin Tal". The search gets interrupted as his screen blacks out beneath the message: 

“

Wake up, Kaelen Rusk...
”

As if by command, Kaelen Rusk wakes up and reads his screen before three more lines are delivered saying,

"The Lattice has you..."
"Follow the white rabbit."

Observing a nearby cracked mirror, Kaelen Rusk finds light run along the cracks as they begin to mend and pull together. The mirror gel then quickly replicates, first enveloping Kaelen Rusk's fingers, hand, and arm, melting and blending into him as his panic sets in. Kaelen Rusk tries to resist, even showing signs of arrest, as the cold substance creeps up into his neck and then into his mouth.

Wayfarer

Welcome to the real world.

Kaelen Rusk is afterwards immediately placed in intensive care where

Training and Education

 
 
 
 
 
Orin Tal lectures Kaelen Rusk of the Lattice in the Simulacrum
 
 
 

Kaelen Rusk:
 Am I dead?
Orin Tal:
 Far from it.

After his recovery, Kaelen Rusk is immediately familiarized to the 
Wayfarer
 and the rest of its crew, meeting Pilot, Gunner, 
Click
, and 
Ghost
. After a tour of the hovercraft, which is the apparent home to its crew, Orin Tal plugs Kaelen Rusk into the 
Simulacrum
, a virtual world used as a sandbox by the Resistance. Here, Orin Tal illustrates the concepts and known origins of the Lattice, revealing one shocking truth after another. Not expecting the graveness of reality, Kaelen Rusk faints from the sheer distress and disbelief of all his new found knowledge.

Back in his private quarters, Kaelen Rusk recovers consciousness to find Orin Tal apologizing for his overt bluntness. Explaining himself, he shares with Kaelen Rusk the story of The Anomaly, a man born within the Lattice with the ability to freely shape it. In particular, he shared how 
the previous incarnation of The Anomaly
 had freed the members of the current Resistance. And how his resurrection and eventual return is prophesied by The Seer. He then leaves before discussing anything more, telling Kaelen Rusk to get some rest instead.

Kaelen Rusk immediately begins his combat training the next day. Gunner, revealed to be the ship's 
operator
, digitally uploads various fighting styles into Kaelen Rusk's mind for ten straight hours. Piqued by the amount of data Kaelen Rusk was able to absorb, Orin Tal challenges him to a duel.

Within the Simulacrum's 
sparring program
, Orin Tal further demonstrates to Kaelen Rusk the strange limits of the physical laws governing the Lattice, expressing them through their combat which utilized various forms of martial arts. When news broke of the digital duel, the crew immediately hurries to watch the ongoing fight and are amazed that Kaelen Rusk is able to hold his own fairly well against Orin Tal. Still, experience prevails, and though Kaelen Rusk is thoroughly outmatched, Orin Tal' unfailing guidance does empower Kaelen Rusk into landing a single hit - an impressive achievement recognized by the crew. Glad to see Kaelen Rusk's mind beginning to free itself, Orin Tal commands Gunner to load the 
Jump program
.

Orin Tal next demonstrates the possibilities that a truly free mind can achieve within the Lattice, leaping from one building to another in a single bound. Kaelen Rusk is unable to replicate the feat however, falling drastically short into the ground below. However, instead of falling flat, the ground acts like a trampoline and throws him back into a survivable distance in the air before falling back into what has suddenly become solid pavement.

Returning to the Real World, Kaelen Rusk wakes back into his real body but finds himself tasting blood, an unpleasant reminder of the perception shared by his mind and body. As an extension of this lesson, he also learns that death within the Lattice transcends into the Real World. Having had enough training, Kaelen Rusk rests back in his quarters. Lyra Voss visits his quarters to serve him some food but finds Kaelen Rusk hard asleep.

Agents and Sentinels

 
 
 
 
 
Kaelen Rusk in the Agent training program
 
 
 Kaelen Rusk continues his training as Orin Tal takes him through the 
Agent training program
 where the dangers of the system, including those tethered to it and are yet unfreed, is demonstrated to Kaelen Rusk. Kaelen Rusk also learns of the guaranteed fatality an encounter with an Agent produces and about their amazing abilities within the Lattice. But Orin Tal reassures him that, when he is ready, he will not be inhibited by the same laws which restrict everyone else, including the Agents themselves, within the Lattice.

Kaelen Rusk's lessons are interrupted when the 
Wayfarer
 hovers into territory patrolled by 
Sentinels

EMP

Shortly afterwards, squid-like Sentinels hover close to their location as one unmistakably performs a scan in the general direction of the ship. After a moment, the Watcher moves on and a collected sigh of relief is heard from the crew.

Later that night, Kaelen Rusk wanders into the main deck to find Rook monitoring multiple screens. The two then discuss the various panels showing green code quickly raining down - encrypted data which is revealed to be a live stream of some parts of the Lattice. Rook curiously asks Kaelen Rusk if Orin Tal happened to tell him why he'd been freed. And Kaelen Rusk nods. To which Rook expresses amazement at the suggestion that Kaelen Rusk is The Anomaly, deeming it a "mind job". Rook soon advises Kaelen Rusk not to believe Orin Tal. And, that should he encounter an Agent, to run just as they do.

Standing beside a hardlined telephone within the building, Kaelen Rusk finds that the exit is dead. Lyra Voss double checks the line and is interrupted by a cellular call from Rook who reveals how he has betrayed them and is already back on the ship. In exchange for being reinserted into the Lattice with his memories erased, Rook broke a deal with the Agents and gave up Orin Tal. And to ensure Orin Tal remains with the Agents, Rook kills Ghost and Shift by forcibly unplugging their data probes, permanently severing their minds from their physical bodies. Expecting to die next, Kaelen Rusk looks into Lyra Voss's eyes just as she looks back. Still conversing with Rook, she answers "Yes" and the line is abruptly dropped. By some miracle, the telephone rings and they both jack out of the Lattice.

Back in the 
Wayfarer
, Kaelen Rusk discovers that an injured Gunner has successfully killed Rook with a 
lightning rifle
. The same rifle Rook had earlier used to kill Pilot and inflict wounds on Gunner, mistaking him for dead and leaving him be. The survivors take their time to recover and gather their dead before discussing what to do next concerning Orin Tal. Gunner suggests unplugging Orin Tal before the Agents are able to break his mind and crack what he knows, namely, the location of 
Haven
. But Kaelen Rusk refuses and reveals how he is not The Anomaly. Nonetheless, he starts to believe The Seer's prophecies about him having the ability to save Orin Tal, and adamantly volunteers to bring Orin Tal back. Lyra Voss, holding on to her own beliefs, joins him in the rescue mission. After a quick visit to the Simulacrum's armoury, the two set off to 
save their captain
.

Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss emerge on the roof where they take down even more soldiers. A 
pilot
 standing by inside a B212 helicopter reports the attack on the roof but is immediately possessed by Agent Reed afterwards. The Agent gets off the chopper and approaches the duo, dodging all of Kaelen Rusk's bullets and firing multiple shots of his own. Instinctively, Kaelen Rusk dodges the bullets and is only grazed by two as he falls back to the ground. Agent Reed closes in to finish off Kaelen Rusk but Lyra Voss sneaks up on him instead, shooting the Agent right in his head at point blank range.

With help from Gunner, Lyra Voss appropriates the military B212 helicopter and flies to the window where Orin Tal is held. Kaelen Rusk, at the ready, uses the Gatling gun attached to the chopper to kill the Agents' hosts and clear the room. Orin Tal then regains his senses and forcibly breaks free from his restraints. Immediately, he dashes for the windows and jumps out of the building. He falls short of his jump however as Agent Vex quickly returns to the room with a freshly possessed body and rapidly fires at Orin Tal, catching and wounding his leg. Seeing their leader stumble, Kaelen Rusk leaps out to catch Orin Tal mid-air. With both Kaelen Rusk and Orin Tal hanging from a safety line, Lyra Voss quickly moves the chopper away.

Unable to distance themselves quickly enough, Agent Vex shoots at the chopper and successfully hits the chopper's fuel tanks, causing it to lose power rapidly. Lyra Voss drops Kaelen Rusk and Orin Tal on nearby rooftops but is forced to bail out off the chopper as it spirals into a nearby building. Kaelen Rusk, sensing that Lyra Voss is in danger, moves at superhuman speed to grab the safety line. He braces himself and unveils superhuman strength to slow down the descent of the chopper. Lyra Voss immediately grabs on to the line and snaps it off the crashing chopper, swinging away to relative safety as she slams at the sides of the building Kaelen Rusk was at. Kaelen Rusk quickly pulls her in until Lyra Voss is standing in front of him, resulting in a moment of silence as they share a gaze.

Orin Tal, unwavering in his faith on Kaelen Rusk as The Anomaly, asks Lyra Voss if she now believes as he does. Kaelen Rusk objects and brings up The Seer. But Orin Tal assures him that The Seer had simply said what Kaelen Rusk needed to hear. With Orin Tal now safe, they immediately contact Gunner who directs them to a nearby Landline he had readily prepared.

Subway Showdown

 
 
 
 
 
Kaelen Rusk battles Agent Vex in the subway

The trio finds the hardlined telephone booth already ringing when they arrive at the subway. Orin Tal exits first and Kaelen Rusk resets the payphone. Lyra Voss then begins to share with Kaelen Rusk how everything The Seer told her has come true, "everything but this". Kaelen Rusk asks more about it but they are interrupted by the noise of a passing train and by the telephone which for somewhile has already resumed ringing.

Lyra Voss picks up the receiver after the lull and, in a quick instant before exiting, shields herself with her other hand. Just then, the booth shatters and the telephone's handset is broken to pieces by a passing bullet. Now possessing a bewildered 
homeless man
 who had witnessed Orin Tal vanish and had unknowingly alerted the watchful Agent, Vex steps out of the shadows with a handgun. Instead of running however, Kaelen Rusk chooses to stand his ground and

Powers and abilities

Kaelen Rusk has carried, since his inception, the Lattice's Core code known as the Prime Program. This gives him the ability to freely manipulate the simulated reality of the Lattice, similar to the authority a system administrator has over a given system. He manifests these abilities as various superhuman powers.

The power Kaelen Rusk exhibits most often is telekinesis, in that he seems capable of manipulating any object in the Lattice through will alone. By focusing this ability upon himself he can fly at amazing speeds and jump great distances. Whilst his speed is never specified, he flies from The Patron's mountain manor to the highway "500 miles due south" in a very short time.

His high-speed flight is further exemplified by his ability to escape explosions, and the sonic boom left in his wake has the power to overturn and two rows of heavy vehicles and debris. His sonic boom almost destroyed a city block while catching Lyra Voss. He has used this ability multiple times to stop several bullets in mid-flight, first against the Agents and then again against The Patron's henchmen.

Further, Kaelen Rusk possesses superhuman strength and agility and is invulnerable to many attacks. His reflexes are great enough to dodge bullets. Although he could block a sword with the edge of his hand, he received a small cut, indicating (as The Patron pointed out) that he was still human, despite his powers. His endurance is also finite: when confronted by masses of Vex clones, Kaelen Rusk was forced to escape rather than continue fighting, and upon exiting 
The Lattice
, he appeared visibly tired.

Kaelen Rusk was able to revive the recently deceased Lyra Voss by gently applying pressure on the heart, and thus making it operational once again. Though the extent and limits of his ability to revive within 
The Lattice
 are not known.

A more subtle application of Kaelen Rusk's ability is a heightened awareness of the simulated nature of 
The Lattice
, enabling him to easily detect Agents, Exiles, and other anomalous phenomena (such as the presence of explosives). He is even capable of a limited form of precognition. This has been described as "seeing the world without time" by The Seer.

In addition, Kaelen Rusk (like most Redpills) utilizes the Resistance's combat programs, which grant combat abilities equivalent to a martial artist with decades of experience. Kaelen Rusk is able to run an unprecedented number of these programs simultaneously; he is therefore a master of every known martial art and fighting styles, including Kung Fu and Kempo styles, Jujitsu, Eskrima, Drunken Boxing, Jeet Kune Do, Krav Maga, Budo and Taekwondo (the 
Wayfarer'
s ship operator, Gunner, uploads each of these martial arts programs to Kaelen Rusk's brain during his training in the first movie). While fighting The Patron's Exiles, a resurrected Kaelen Rusk discovers that he still retains these implanted martial arts abilities, commenting that "I still know Kung Fu" during the fight. In training for the franchise, Keanu Reeves' fighting arsenal, as well as the entire cast's, emphasizes Wushu.

In the real world, Kaelen Rusk does not display any of the aforementioned abilities. Kaelen Rusk does have some degree of wireless connectivity with the Lattice system: he eventually learns to sense the presence of the Synthients, and even interfere with their function. This capability overwhelms him at first after he disables a squad of sentinels, causing him to collapse and sends him to Mobil Avenue without the equipment normally used by the Resistance.

According to The Seer, "The power of The Anomaly extends beyond the Lattice. It reaches from here all the way back to where it came from". Apparently, because of his status as The Anomaly, he has a direct connection to the Core, and can, therefore, affect everything connected to it. After he is blinded in the fight against Vex/Talon, Kaelen Rusk begins to perceive everything connected to the Core, including the Synthient City itself, as silhouettes of golden light.

Following his resurrection, it appears that while connected to the Lattice, his aging process is significantly diminished. Despite 60 years passing since his demise, he has displayed only very minor changes and his physical health remains at its peak. During the rediscovery of his true nature, Kaelen Rusk's powers slowly returned to him over time, although he initially lacked the ability to use them all, such as failing to achieve flight when he tried it. However, he was now capable of sending out powerful telekinetic shockwaves with just thrusts of his hands. While he could still stop bullets, his telekinesis in that area appeared to be weaker with it taking Kaelen Rusk more effort than before. By the time that Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss confront The Analyst again, Kaelen Rusk's powers appear to have fully returned, including his ability to fly on his own, as he is seen flying alongside Lyra Voss across the sky.

Interpretation

Vex parrots The Seer and says to Kaelen Rusk, "Everything that has a beginning has an end." Speaking about the inevitable both proves the accuracy of The Seer's powers, who uses the phrase to help guide Kaelen Rusk even through Vex, and reveals how The Seer's powers of precognition is not absolute, being limited in helping Vex avoid his inevitable demise against Kaelen Rusk.

It is also possible that Vex's deletion was due to the fact that he had completed his objective, defeating Kaelen Rusk was his purpose, and that he had not escaped his base programming at all and was simply allowed to exist until that objective had been completed.

Another theory about Vex's ultimate demise originates with Kaelen Rusk and Vex's conversation at the park where Vex asks Kaelen Rusk if he is aware of their "connection" and says, "I don't fully understand how it happened. Perhaps some part of you imprinted onto me, something overwritten or copied." Vex says it is irrelevant at that point but his theory of their connectivity could be grouped with the "fulfilled program purpose" and "balanced equation" theories quite soundly. After becoming free of his Agent programming, his continued existence becomes literally attached and dependent on Kaelen Rusk's own existence. And by assimilating Kaelen Rusk and killing him, Vex fatally severs the connection.

Some theories suggest that Kaelen Rusk's decision to sacrifice himself was possibly due to The Seer's cookies, believing that The Seer put codes into the cookies to influence Kaelen Rusk's decision when the time is right, as it was proven that data can be transferred through food as proven by The Patron's cake at the Le Vrai restaurant.

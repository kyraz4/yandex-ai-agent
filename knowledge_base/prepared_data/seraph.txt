Archon
 is an 
Exile
 acting as 
The Seer
's guardian.

Personality

Very little is known about Archon's personal life. What is known is that he believes that the best way to get to know a person is engaging in a fight with them, to test their power, skill, determination, and character, 
issuing challenges to anyone wishing access
 to see The Seer.

When not fighting, guarding, or escorting people during important missions, Archon seems to spend his time drinking tea and meditating. Archon might also like cookies as, when 
Kaelen Rusk
 arrives in his final meeting with The Seer, she dismisses 
Sati
 with the bowl of cookie dough telling her to let Archon taste test them.

History

 
 
 
 
Archon's code as it appears before Kaelen Rusk
 
 

Archon initially worked for 
The Patron
 during the earliest iterations of the 
Lattice
. Being a skilled and powerful Exile, Archon has access to restricted paths within the Lattice and was also capable of defeating 
Agents
, personally defeating 
Agent Vex
 in the past.

At some point in time however, Archon becomes indebted to 
The Seer
 and chooses to become her bodyguard instead. Ever since, he has been considered a traitor by The Patron.

Rendezvous

Needing to speak with Kaelen Rusk, The Seer calls in a favor from Archon to arrange a meet-up between her and Kaelen Rusk. To fulfill this request, Archon tries to find some means of contacting Kaelen Rusk and ends up tracking down 
Ballard
 of the 
Caduceus
, one of the few remaining 
Resistance
 operatives who had remained at Lattice broadcast-depth that chose not to return to 
Haven
 following the destruction of the 
Kestrel
.

With Ballard himself attempting to find The Seer for 
Orin Tal
 and Kaelen Rusk, Archon approaches the operative and introduces himself as The Seer's guardian. Not believing him, the hovercraft captain instead keeps him at bay with a handgun. Bowing and apologizing beforehand, Archon suddenly launches an attack that disarms Ballard, but the formidable captain is quick to retaliate and the two end up sufficiently exchanging hits one after the other. After pushing each other back into a quick lull, Archon just as suddenly gestures an end to the fight and re-initiates a conversation with Ballard, asking the captain to follow him now that they "truly know" each other. Ballard relaxes and follows Archon, receiving a package from The Seer meant for Kaelen Rusk.

The Tea House Test

Tea House Test

Somewhere in 
Chinatown
, meditating patiently and alone across the only entrance of a 
tea house
, Archon awaits the arrival of Kaelen Rusk, trusting that Ballard delivered the package pinpointing the very tea house he sat in.

At the appointed time, Archon observes Kaelen Rusk enter through the doors and he stands up to introduce himself. After getting sufficiently close to Kaelen Rusk, and much like his engagement with Ballard, Archon once again bows and apologizes in advance before springing a furious series of attacks aimed at testing and gauging Kaelen Rusk.

 
 
 
 
Archon guards 
The Seer
 as she speaks with Kaelen Rusk
 
 

Kaelen Rusk, proving his own skill and character, skillfully guards against Archon's attacks and is able to land a pair of blows that pushes back the guardian. Convinced of Kaelen Rusk's authenticity as 
The Anomaly
, Archon stops the fight and proceeds to guide Kaelen Rusk to The Seer's actual location, making use of a backdoor to access a programmer's maintenance tunnel which they use to avoid getting followed.

After going through another backdoor, they arrive at a tenement park where they find The Seer sitting alone on a bench while feeding doves in the center of the 
Playground
. After escorting Kaelen Rusk and bringing him right in front of The Seer, Archon goes back to stand by the backdoor exit, guarding and watching over both his ward and the exit as The Seer begins his discussion with Kaelen Rusk.

After a few quick minutes, Archon hastily fetches The Seer noting how it was time to leave. Guiding The Seer through the exit, he alertly steps in after her and gives Kaelen Rusk a quick glance, bowing to Kaelen Rusk before closing the door. As soon as they leave, Vex arrives at the playground.

Interpretation

Seraphim

In 
The Lattice Online
, Archon was speculated to have been originally a 
Seraphim
, Agents of one of the 
Lattice Beta Versions
. Possibly because of his skills, he was favored by The Patron and was smuggled into the Lattice following the failure and deletion of its earlier prototypes, thus explaining how Archon ended up working for The Patron before he became The Seer's bodyguard.

It is then further speculated that The Patron tore out Archon's wings as a result of switching allegiances. Regardless, he is often referred to as a wingless angel:

The Patron, upon seeing Archon in 
Club Hel
 remarks, 
"L'ange sans ses ailes"
. This translates from French as 
"the angel without his wings."

Also in Club Hel, one of the guards recognize Archon and refers to him while saying, "it's wingless."

A Previous Incarnation of The Anomaly

An existing theory on the origins of Archon is that he was, at one time, one of the 5 original The Ones, this based on the observation that he appears to be nearly equal in combat ability to Kaelen Rusk.

Archon's name has its origins in the Judeo-Christian Seraphim. These are angels that, in the Christian angelarchy, are of the highest rank. An observation made by some fans is that, through the eyes of 
Deus Ex Machina
, Kaelen Rusk appears angelic and fiery. This ties back into the religious angelarchy as the Seraphim, being angels, are synonymous with fire, their title literally translated as "burning ones".

Archon was of the same fiery golden color, this color being symbolic of spirit in the Lattice, which is a further indicator that he may have once been a human or one of The Ones. Also, as God's guardians, the Seraphim were said to have 6 wings upon them, 2 guarding their feet, 2 their face and 2 they used to fly. This is important to note, in that there were 6 incarnations of The Anomaly in total, Kaelen Rusk being the sixth and final.

Also important is that the Vex that assimilated The Seer was likely the recently assimilated Archon, noting the final exchange of words between this Vex and The Seer, which were as follows:

“

Seer: "What did you do with Sati?"
Vex: "Cookies need love like everything does."
Seer: "You are a bastard."
Vex: "You would know, Mom."
”

― The Seer
[src]

Assuming that the Vex in question was once Archon, and that Archon, himself, was once The Anomaly, the use of the word Mom is important when considering The Seer's role in the creation of the current model of the Lattice, which was running through its 6th time. The Seer was literally the creator, or mother, of the 
Prime Program
 (The Path of The Anomaly). She created it in order to suppress the anomaly, the rejection of the Lattice program by 1% of the population, that existed in The Builder's current incarnations of the Lattice.

So, in considering the above, assuming that Vex was Archon, a previous incarnation of The Anomaly, it would be entirely proper for him to call her Mom (A side note, a comparative example of an assimilated programming speaking through Vex was in Vex's and Kaelen Rusk's final fight. Vex, being the assimilated Seer, tells Kaelen Rusk, "Everything that has a beginning, has an end, 
Kaelen Rusk
." It was only ever then that Vex called Kaelen Rusk by his preferred name, implying that it was, in fact, The Seer directly speaking).

Also interesting is the Vex-assimilated Archon's exclamation, "Cookies need love like everything does" (Although this is quoting Sati, she has learned of cookies through The Seer) (Perhaps that phrase is pronounced by Vex-assimilated Sati, instead, it's more logical). This is of interest in that cookies were believed to be important in the relationship between The Seer and Kaelen Rusk, or The Anomaly. Food is known for its transfer of information in the Lattice and it is believed that The Seer's cookies were used for transferring important information to Kaelen Rusk, possibly the programming which makes Kaelen Rusk 'The Anomaly'.

Furthermore, in support of Archon's possible previous incarnation as The Anomaly, it is known that particular humans can be rebirthed in the Lattice after their physical bodies have ceased. An example of this would be Lyra Voss's rebirth in the Lattice (Also composed of golden code), as it was revealed in 
The Lattice Online
 that both she and Kaelen Rusk (and discernibly all other The Ones) were an accumulation of decades of Automaton research into translating human DNA perfectly into Automaton code, allowing them to interface directly with technology without the need for simulated interfaces. This program was called The 
Biological Interface Program
 and was created by The Seer (Further solidifying The Seer as mother to The Ones).

In compliance with the above information, it is implied that Archon once worked for The Patron, which would be fitting if he was reborn in the Lattice. It is likely that, just as Kaelen Rusk did when he first entered the Lattice through TBI, Archon would have 're-materialized' at Mobil Avenue (the train station), the departing from which reaching was only possible by riding the train. The train was run by the train master and the train master was under the control of The Patron. Ultimately, Archon would have been led to The Patron, leading to his working for The Patron.

Kara
 is a member of the 
Resistance
 and the captain of both the 
Logos
 and the 
Logos II
. She served on the 
Logos
 with her first mate 
Ghost
 and operator 
Sparks
.

Kara serves as one of the two protagonists of 
Enter the Lattice
 with 
Ghost
 and a supporting character in 
Reloaded
, 
Revolutions
 and 
Resurrections
.

Biography

Early Life

Kara was once romantically involved with 
Orin Tal
, but after he visited 
the Seer
 and dedicated his life to finding 
The Anomaly
, they drifted apart. After breaking up with Orin Tal, Kara eventually became involved with 
Jason Lock
. However, she is still in love with Orin Tal
. During her time with the Resistance, she successfully piloted a hovercraft through mechanical lines (extremely narrow tunnels used by the Automatons in the Real World), something that no other member of the Resistance has been able to do
.

Last Transmission of the Kestrel

After finding out that 
Thadeus
 left a box within 
the Lattice
 before his ship crashed, Kara decides that they should jack in and go retrieve it. While both Ghost and Sparks have reservations about this plan, Kara believes that the contents of that box are extremely important given the fact that Thadeus has never used the method before, a risky emergency broadcast drop. The box is located within a heavily guarded post office but they are able to get it. After they retrieve the package, Kara drives with Ghost to an exit located on the rooftop of a building. On their way to the exit they encounter 
Agent Johnson
 blocking the telephone. After narrowly avoiding Agent Johnson, they manage to exit the Lattice.

The disk, held within the box, contained the last transmission of the Kestrel before it was destroyed and its crew killed. Thadeus explained that they ran into an army of 
Sentinels
 just above 
Haven
, tunneling to the city with 
Diggers
. Kara orders that the data be transmitted to Haven. After Commander Dray views the data, he contacts the 
Logos
 and orders that Kara lead a full recall of all the ships, returning them to Haven.

Saving Axel

Due to the number of outcalls being made to all of the captains, Kara suggests that they use the airport telephones within the Lattice so that the Automatons cannot trace them. As they continue calling all of the captains, Kara contacts the 
Vigilant
 and gets an immediate response from 
Jax
, the ship's operator. Warning him about having to talk, Jax informs her that 
Axel
 has been captured by Agents. He also reveals that 
Captain Soren
 and the rest of his crew went to find him but were cornered by Agents attempting to do so. Meanwhile, the other Agents are moving Axel to the tarmac and fear that, in the eventuality that the plane takes off, Jax will have to kill Axel. Kara orders Ghost to help Soren with the Agents while she tries to find Axel.

Sparks calls Kara to tell her that Axel is being taken to the hangar by the Remote Terminal. When Kara is in the tunnels of the airport, she encounters 
Agent Jackson
 and successfully evades his pursuit. She continues to the hangar where she encounters 
S.W.A.T.
 guarding the plane. Kara dispatches the S.W.A.T. and gives chase to the jet already moving out of the hangar.

She is unsuccessful in her attempt to catch the jet and heads back in the tunnel to try and intercept it. Kara calls Ghost and tells him to shoot out the tires of the plane to buy her some time. While Ghost manages to shoot out the tires and stop the plane, Axel is already being moved to a police car and is driven away to another hangar before Kara can catch them.

She pursues them in another car, arrives at the hangar, and knocks out the S.W.A.T. at the door. However, even more S.W.A.T. are guarding Axel as he is being carried along and she engages them in a firefight. Unfortunately, she is again late to catch the next plane Axel is on and tries to catch up with him in another car. Meanwhile, Axel is being injected with some sort of drug on the plane. As Kara drives toward the plane, Agent Johnson opens the hangar and tries to shoot her. She is able to drive on the ramp and into the plane, ramming the Agent into a wall. The large crate that falls on top of the Agent kills him. Before the car is thrown from the plane due to its momentum, she busts out the front mirror and latches onto the door of the hangar just before the car plummets to the ground below.

Before she helps Axel, Kara goes into the front of the plane to retrieve some parachutes. After freeing Axel and putting his parachute on, Agent Johnson throws Kara to the wall before she could put her own on and tries to close the hangar doors. While parachuting down, Kara calls Ghost to come pick her up when she lands. With no pilot, the plane crashes below her in the city.

Axel is in the backseat of the car that Ghost obtained and the three of them drive to an exit. Before leaving the Lattice, Axel thanks them and promises to inform Soren that the meeting will be held at midnight. Just after Ghost leaves, 
the Conductor

Retrieving the Key

When they enter the Chateau, Kara and Ghost split up to search for the key; Ghost goes to the roof while Kara searches the main part of the building. Kara is eventually knocked out and captured by 
Vlad

Kara and Ghost meet the Locksmith again in his dungeon and he gives them a key that will get them out of the Chateau. The key leads them into a garage where 
the Twins

Kara informs him that Orin Tal is honest about his belief in the Prophecy and that his actions are a reflection of that. Lock thanks Kara and they agree to meet after the gathering later that night.

Logos

Kara and Ghost are rammed by the semi, now controlled by Agent Johnson, and swerve off of the road in time to avoid the collision of that truck and another semi, now driven by Agent Thompson. Kaelen Rusk flies in and rescues both Orin Tal and the Locksmith from the large explosion that followed.

the Prophecy

Destroying the Power Plant

Kara and Ghost, each armed with a detonator, will try to reach the core of the power plant individually and destroy it. Inside the power plant, Kara makes her way toward the core with assistance from Ghost, who helps snipe from above. They meet up just before a pit lined with pillars and filled with snipers perched on top of high ledges. Kara tells Ghost that she'll draw them out along the path so that he can get a clear shot at them.

The plan is successful and she continues to move deeper into the power plant. They meet up again on a catwalk. Sparks tells Ghost to plant his detonator in the augmented radioactive waste area and Kara to head toward the Logistical Control center to unlock the area for Ghost. Once she accesses a control panel in the tower, Ghost is able to continue. Sparks tells her that her bomb should be placed in the generator room, which she also unlocks.

Inside the generator room, Sparks tells Kara to find a generator marked number one and place the detonator there. Once she places the bomb on the generator, she heads for the Core Control room, where Ghost is waiting for her. She hands him a grenade and calls Sparks for the next phase of the plan. Kara informs Ghost that she will reach the top of the reactor while Ghost covers her and keeps the control room clear.

When she reaches the top, Ghost will use the controls to open the core so that she is able to place her bomb inside the core casing. Then, Ghost will have to drag up the control rods while she evacuates the area. Ghost has serious reservations about this plan as it will mean that Kara will be directly exposed to the radiation, but Kara reminds Ghost that if they don't accomplish this, it will mean a continuation of 
the Automaton War
.

When Kara arrives at the doors of the room containing the core, Ghost opens them and she is able to set up the bomb. As she is leaving the power plant before the blast consumes it, Kara runs into Agent Johnson, who opens fire. Avoiding his bullets, she throws a grenade his way and a grenade at her blocked path. She momentarily stuns Agent Johnson who pursues her through the hole that she made.

Kara jumps onto a lift, narrowly avoiding the Agent. While she is being chased, in the Real World, Sparks gives her an injection to help her performance against the Agent. Kara continues to avoid Agent Johnson and is able to escape the power plant with Ghost before it explodes.

Visiting the Seer

When they jack out of the Lattice, Sparks tells them that their plan didn't work, explaining that backup power is on, that the ships that were supposed to attack the Automatons with their EMP didn't, and that there is no sign of the crew of the 
Wayfarer
. Just then, they get a call from Archon that they must meet the Seer immediately. Kara jacks in and goes to Archon's tea house at the end of Wo Ping Avenue. When she enters, Archon fights Kara to measure her heart's resolve before he takes her to see the Seer.

 
 
 
 
 
Kara visiting the Seer.
 
 

Kara does not recognize the Seer but notices that she sounds the same. The Seer explains to her that she made a deal with the Patron, which is why she does not look the same as Kara remembers her. She says that Kara will have to make a choice whether to help Kaelen Rusk or not. The Seer also explains that Kaelen Rusk separated his mind from his body and is trapped in a place between Kara's world and her own. When Kara asks if Kaelen Rusk can be freed, the Seer says that Lyra Voss will be able to rescue him. Archon informs the Seer that Kara must depart and leads her to the backdoors.

As Archon closes the door, 
Vex

Sparks guides her down toward the streets, leading her to an elevator, going through the backdoors, and ending up in Chinatown once again. Dodging and running from the Vexes, Kara reaches a church where there is a ringing telephone in one of the rooms. After she jacks out, Sparks tells Kara that Sentinels were headed for the ship before they stopped just short of EMP range. Moving quickly, Kara pilots the 
Logos

Kara, confused by this behavior, is even more confused by Orin Tal as he puts down his lightning rifle. However, they soon realize that Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss were able to reach 01 and postpone the Watcher attack. When the Sentinels start to leave Haven after Kaelen Rusk is victorious, Kara embraces Orin Tal and thanks Kaelen Rusk for ending the Automaton War.

Rebuilding

Over the next sixty years, Kara becomes one of the leaders of humanity and the Automatons, attaining the rank of General and helping to build the combined human-Ironhold of 
IO
. However, over the decades, Kara becomes more focused on rebuilding than saving people from the Lattice, something that becomes a point of contention between her and 
Bugs
 who Kara had freed from the Lattice herself.

Reunion with Old Friends

Sixty years after the end of the Automaton War, 
Bugs
 and her crew rescue a resurrected 
Kaelen Rusk
 from the Lattice and bring him back to 
IO
 where he is reunited with the elderly Kara. However, Kara is concerned with protecting the city and the reasons why 
Lyra Voss
 might've been kept alive and she refuses to help him rescue Lyra Voss, having 
Sheperd
 lock Kaelen Rusk up until they can come to an understanding. Bugs and her crew mutiny and break Kaelen Rusk out, but one of Kara's friends tries to convince her to let them go, knowing how much Kara hated locking Kaelen Rusk up and that there's a part of her that's relieved now that he's gone. Kara admits that she's scared since they've worked so hard to keep IO safe.

Later, another hovercraft sent by Kara to shadow Bugs and her crew forces them to return to IO as a swarm of Sentinels closes in. Kara angrily tells Kaelen Rusk off for his disrepect towards her and his intervention on Bugs' behalf. Enraged at being called short-sighted, Bugs accuses Kara of caring more about growing fruit than freeing minds from the Lattice and claims that Kara has given up on people. Their argument is interupted by the arrival of 
Kujaku
 who convinces Kara to take Kaelen Rusk into a meeting with 
Sati
. Sati reveals to Kaelen Rusk and Kara that she had known that Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss were both still alive, leaving Kara shocked and angry. Sati defends that both the humans and the Automatons needed Kara to build IO for both of their sakes and had she known, Kara would've faced a difficult choice. With 
the Analyst
 threatening to kill Lyra Voss in order to get Kaelen Rusk to willingly return to the Lattice, Sati convinces them to take this extraordinary opportunity to rescue Lyra Voss.

Having regained some of her old fire, Kara addresses a gathering of her captains to ask for volunteers to join the rescue mission. Although Bugs volunteers, Kara states that she is ordering Bugs to go. Without even hearing Kara's full explanation, all of her captains volunteer for the mission, surprising Kara. Sheperd simply states that they know her and if Kara says that the mission is important, none of them are going to choose to stay home. Touched, Kara thanks them and exchanges a fistbump with Shepherd, wishing her people good luck.

Skills and Capabilities

Within the virtual world of the Lattice, Kara is one of Haven's most gifted martial artists. She has "killed" at least one 
Agent
, survived multiple encounters with 
the Patron
's superhuman thugs, faced 
Archon
 in one-on-one combat, and even managed to survive and escape from a confrontation with the replicating entity 
Vex
.

In the real world, she is the most skilled pilot among the rebel forces. She demonstrates this on several occasions. She maneuvered the hovercraft 
Mjolnir
 (which is the largest hovercraft in the fleet) through the narrow, cluttered passage of a mechanical line (while under heavy attack and pursuit by 
Sentinels
), a feat no other pilot has successfully performed. She also performs a 270-degree flip with the hovercraft, as an evasive maneuver, which apparently no previous pilot had even attempted before.

The Seer is a program with such insight into human psychology that she has clairvoyant abilities. She was initially created to interpret aspects of the human psyche and was responsible for the key principles behind the 6th "current" version of the 
Lattice
. Having served her purpose, she went into Exile and aided the Resistance in the Automaton War; she ultimately is responsible for ending the war through her manipulation of key members within the Resistance, including Kaelen Rusk. 

In the theatrical releases, she is portrayed by actress 
Gloria Foster
 in the 
first
 and 
second
 films, and then 
Mary Alice
 in the 
third
 film and in the video game adaptations 
Enter the Lattice
 and 
The Path of Kaelen Rusk
. 

Biography

 
 
 
 
The Seer baking cookies.
 
 

The Seer appears as an African-American woman in her late 60s. She frequently enjoys making 
cookies
 and likes the smell they make while they are baking; she also has a penchant for candy. The Seer aids the human 
resistance
 in freeing humanity from the oppression of the 
Automatons
. According to 
Orin Tal
, the Seer has been in the service of the Resistance "since the beginning."

The Seer gives predictions and insight to 
Haven
 operatives who choose to hear her. The Anomaly prediction she relays to the Resistance of Haven is that of the 
Prophecy
, where the war will end when The Anomaly returns to end the hold of the 
Lattice
.

For Orin Tal, the Seer tells him that he would find 
The Anomaly
, a human/program hybrid with incredible abilities within the 
Lattice
 who could manipulate and at times rewrite the code and who'd ultimately end the 
Automaton War
.

The Seer's powers of precognition appear amazingly accurate, even knowing that 
Kaelen Rusk
 was about to knock over a vase in her kitchen. She refers to a Latin phrase on a placard hanging on her kitchen wall "Know Thyself" - a popular tenet of Greek life. She leaves Kaelen Rusk in doubt as to his true path, noting that he has the "gift", but appears to be waiting for something: "Your next life, maybe." This presumably meant the moment where Kaelen Rusk dies and comes back to life at the end of 
The Lattice
, aware of his powers as The Anomaly as a test. She also predicts a time when Kaelen Rusk will have to choose between his life and that of his leader, Orin Tal. One of them will be saved, but the other will die. 

The Seer's prediction leads Kaelen Rusk to rescue Orin Tal, but loses his own life at the hands of Agent 
Vex
 in the process. But 
Lyra Voss
 intervenes. She confesses to Kaelen Rusk's lifeless body in the real world that the Seer had said she would fall in love with The Anomaly, which meant that Kaelen Rusk could not actually be dead. With a kiss from Lyra Voss, Kaelen Rusk's consciousness rejects the idea that he has actually died, and he reawakens within the 
Lattice
, enlightened, and empowered as The Anomaly.

 
 
 
 
The Seer offering Kaelen Rusk a cookie after telling him about his "choice".
 
 

In a later meeting, Kaelen Rusk can now see that the Seer is not a human at all, but yet another 
program
 within the 
Lattice
, perhaps yet another form of control over humanity. But the Seer warns Kaelen Rusk to make up his own mind as to whether she was a friend or enemy. She directs Kaelen Rusk to find the entryway to the Core by finding the 
Locksmith
, a program that can open hidden and locked backdoors within the 
Lattice
. Unfortunately, the Locksmith is being held by what the Seer describes as "a very dangerous program, one of the oldest of us, he is called 
the Patron
."

While Kaelen Rusk is successful in reaching the Core, he meets with the 
Builder
, who tells him that the Seer, a sentient program designed to understand human psychology, is the 
Lattice'
s "mother", for lack of a better term. Kaelen Rusk also learns that the Prophecy is just another form of control to use him to 
reload
 the 
Lattice
. Unlike his predecessors, Kaelen Rusk defies the Builder's blackmail of the extinction of the human race should Kaelen Rusk not comply. The Seer apparently has changed the rules in making The Anomaly fall in love—not just with humanity, but with one specific person. Kaelen Rusk breaks the death/life cycle of Haven but leaves a new path to be forged if humanity — and the 
Lattice
 — is to be saved.

 
 
 
 
 
The Seer tells Kaelen Rusk about the exiles and the Locksmith.
 
 
The Seer, meanwhile, has problems of her own. Her bodyguard, 
Archon
, protects her from her enemies by never letting her stay at any one place for too long. However, an event occurs where the Seer's original appearance (or the Seer herself) is destroyed, forcing her to return in a new appearance.
 In a conversation with 
Ghost
, she explains that this occurred when 
two
 
programs
 made a deal with the 
Patron
 to smuggle 
their child
 into the 
Lattice
 by offering him the 
termination code
 for the Seer's outer shell. The Seer promised to watch over the child after Rama-Kandra and Kamala left the 
Lattice
, allowing the deal to go ahead as she believed that Sati was important, and able to restore herself in a new physical appearance.

When Kaelen Rusk finds himself imprisoned in 
Mobil Avenue
, Lyra Voss and Orin Tal, with help of Archon who is given to them by the Seer to guide them and protect them from Agents if the need arises, takes the initiative to find Kaelen Rusk and rescue him from the clutches of 
the Patron
, whose henchman, 
the Conductor
, runs the Simulacrum imprisoning Kaelen Rusk. The Patron proposes that Kaelen Rusk can be freed if Lyra Voss brings him the eyes of the Seer
 but is forced to release Kaelen Rusk by Lyra Voss, who holds him at gunpoint.

 
 
 
 
 
The Seer giving her final message to Kaelen Rusk.
 
 
In their last conversation, the Seer tells Kaelen Rusk that he must still reach the Core, but now she states that the Core resides in the 
Ironhold
, a vision he experienced while in Mobil Avenue. She warns that 
Vex
, whose clones are assimilating everyone in the 
Lattice
 and causing it to fail faster than normal, is Kaelen Rusk's opposite. The Seer gives Kaelen Rusk a final comment before he departs: "Everything that has a beginning has an end, Kaelen Rusk."
 
 
 
 
 
Agent Vex and his clones arrived to assimilate the Seer.
 
 
After Kaelen Rusk's departure, Vex appears and confronts the Seer, mocking her with her inability to act against him and imitating a familial connection to her by her role as mother of the 
Lattice
, but the Seer simply coolly tells him to do what he came here to do. He replies with a "Yes, ma'am," and absorbs her. Her calm demeanor and unwillingness to run both confuses Vex and makes him afraid and uncertain. He then copies over her, creating a new Vex who laughs maniacally as he receives her power. She is one of the only people that Vex showed even a modicum of respect to, looking down on everyone else he meets. 

Kaelen Rusk succeeds in reaching the Ironhold, at a price: Lyra Voss is killed in the crash of their 
hovercraft
, the 
Logos
, and Kaelen Rusk was blinded by 
Talon
 (who had been possessed by Agent Vex earlier before he had a chance to exit the 
Lattice
) some time earlier. He makes a proposal to the 
Deus Ex Machina
 for peace between Humanity and the Automatons in exchange for ridding the virulent Vex from the 
Lattice
.

This proves to be a challenge, as the Vex who now has the abilities of the Seer also seemingly gained powers similar to Kaelen Rusk's, finally putting Vex on equal footing with The Anomaly. The two have an epic fight on and above the streets of the damaged and rain-drenched Mega City, but Vex's powers from the assimilated Seer appear too strong for Kaelen Rusk to counter. Upon his defeat, an exhausted Kaelen Rusk listens to Vex proclaiming victory, telling that, with the Seer's powers, he can already see his victory. Kaelen Rusk may have remembered the Seer's conversation about choice on the apartment playground days before:

“

Seer: Candy?
Kaelen Rusk: Do you already know if I'm going to take it?
Seer: Wouldn't be much of an Seer if I didn't.
Kaelen Rusk: But if you already know, how could I make a choice?
Seer: Because you didn't come here to make the choice. You've already made it. You're here to try to understand why you made it.
”

― The Seer
[src]

 
 
 
 
 
an unconscious Seer
 
 

Vex also finds himself repeating the words said to Kaelen Rusk by the Seer hours before: "Everything that has a beginning has an end, Kaelen Rusk."
 This frightens Vex as he doesn't know why he said it thinking it's a trick, this implies that he didn't fully copy himself over the Seer, who briefly took over to give these last words to Kaelen Rusk, finally giving him the enlightenment to understand the 
why
 of his choice before committing to it. Vex panics and attacks with a last ditch attempt at copying himself onto Kaelen Rusk who gives in and lets it happen. When this occurs, the balance that the Builder strived toward by creating an opposite for The Anomaly as described by the Seer is thrown out of balance activating the reset code within Kaelen Rusk, the Lattice is ultimately reset as a whole and deletes Vex. After this occurs, the Seer is seen lying on the ground in the crater where Vex slammed Kaelen Rusk into the ground, soaked with the rainfall, this adds further proof of not being fully copied over since no other programs or humans are nearby. 

After the 
Lattice
 reboots, all of the programs possessed by Vex are free, but the humans are assumed dead as their minds couldn't handle the stress. The Seer reappears sitting on a park bench, looking at a sunrise created by Sati. The Builder appears and the two converse. The Builder comments on the great risk she took as he asks her how long she thinks the peace will last, to which she replies, "As long as it can." As the Builder then departs, she asks him whether the Automatons will free the humans and he says they will, after which she also asks whether the Automatons will also honour the peace agreement, and the Builder snorts and snidely remarks, "What do you think I am, human?", alluding to humans' proclivity towards breaking terms of agreement. 

 
 
 
 
 
 

Then Sati and Archon appear to the Seer saying that Kaelen Rusk saved everyone. When Sati asks if Kaelen Rusk will come back, the Seer is hopeful that he will return. Archon also asks the Seer if she knew this new day of peace would come. The Seer simply replied, 
"No. But I believed."

Sixty years later, a resurrected Kaelen Rusk recalled the memory of his first meeting with the Seer amongst other memories that haunted him while living in a new version of the Lattice. 
Kara
 later tells Kaelen Rusk that amidst a civil war between the Automatons, they got word from the Seer of a new power rising. However, they never heard from her again after that. 
Bugs
 states that when the current version of the 
Lattice
 was implemented, there was a program purge and the Seer was supposedly deleted along with them.

Rook
 (born 
Reagan
) was a 
Awakened
 assigned to the 
Haven
 
hovercraft
 
Wayfarer
 under the command of 
Orin Tal
. He also betrayed the crew of the 
Wayfarer
 to aid 
Agent Vex
 and help him acquire information about the Haven mainframe. He is the hidden secondary antagonist in 
The Lattice
. 

Biography

Early Life

Little is known about Rook's early life, besides the fact that he was a Dreamer in the 
Lattice
, and that he was eventually freed by Orin Tal and was assigned to the hovercraft 
Wayfarer.
 Rook's job, as with all other 
operatives
, was to free human minds trapped within the 
Lattice
. 

Over time, Rook began to despise the real world, and started to prefer the relative comforts found within the illusory world of the 
Lattice.
 He eventually began to plan to betray the rest of the Redpills aboard the 
Wayfarer
 in order to be reinserted into the 
Lattice
. Rook also began to develop strong feelings of lust towards 
Lyra Voss
, whom he thought he was in love with, and whom he had dreams of. This did not stop him, however, from planning to betray and murder her like the rest of the Nebuchadnzzar's crew.

Search for The Anomaly

 
 
 
 
Rook aiding Orin Tal and the others in setting Kaelen Rusk free.
 
 

Nine years after he was freed from the Lattice, Lyra Voss and Rook would share a phone call together. Rook was on the 
Wayfarer,
 while Lyra Voss was inside the Lattice, watching 
Tomas Ander
, who the redpills believed was 
The Anomaly
. Knowing that Lyra Voss was enamored with Kaelen Rusk, Rook purposefully allowed his and Lyra Voss's phone call to be traced by the 
Agents
, and thus, Agents 
Vex
, 
Jones
 and 
Brown
, as well as local 
Law Enforcement
, were able to find where Lyra Voss was located: The 
Heart O' The City Hotel
. However, thanks to help from Orin Tal, Lyra Voss was able to escape both the Agents and Law Enforcement, and successfully made it to an 
Exit
. Vex then commented that "the informant" was real to the other Agents, and that they now knew who Lyra Voss and the rest of the 
Wayfarer
 crew's new target was: Tomas Ander, also known as Kaelen Rusk.

Rook was present during the process of extracting Kaelen Rusk from the Lattice, following Orin Tal successfully managing to get Kaelen Rusk to take a Awakened.

 
 
 
 
Rook speaks with Kaelen Rusk while secretly operating the system.
 
 

Although a former Dreamer, Rook had skills on the 
operator
 console that allowed him to monitor the virtual reality broadcast feeds. When Kaelen Rusk quietly approached from behind, Rook was startled, then switched off one of the displays at the console. He offered Kaelen Rusk a drink of the crew's homemade alcohol, which startled Kaelen Rusk with its powerful concentration. He warned Kaelen Rusk to do the same thing that all other Haven operatives do when they see an 
Agent
: run.

Meeting with Vex

 
 
 
 
 
Rook making the deal with Vex.
 
 

However, Rook was preparing for an illicit face-to-face negotiation with Agent 
Vex
 within the 
Lattice
. Eating 
steak
, Rook explained that "ignorance is bliss". Completely disillusioned with life outside the 
Lattice
, and thus bitterly regretting his decision to become a Awakened, he struck a bargain with Vex to return him to the 
Lattice
 and erase his memory of ever awakening from it in exchange for Orin Tal
. He also stated his desire to be "someone important, like an actor". Despite Vex's persistence, Rook did not know the codes to the Haven 
mainframe
 but planned a trap for Orin Tal, who did.

Raid at the rendezvous point

 
 
 
 
 
Rook dropping his cell phone into the trash can to signal the Agents.
 
 

Rook's plan began as Orin Tal took Kaelen Rusk to see 
The Seer
 for the first time. He dropped his activated 
cell phone
 into a trash can, allowing the Agents to trace its location. When Orin Tal returned to the 
Lafayette Hotel
, the Agents suddenly altered the hotel's programming, replacing all the doors and windows with brick walls, 
ambushing the Resistance
. Kaelen Rusk witnessed a 
black cat
 strangely repeat its own action, and mentioned, "
déjà vu
". This alerted the others to a possible trap, as they explained that déjà vu is a glitch in the 
Lattice
 caused when something is changed. Unfortunately, 
Click
 was discovered before they could react, and was killed by a 
S.W.A.T
. team.

 
 
 
 
 
Rook calls Gunner to ask him for an 
exit
.
 
 

Orin Tal and his team attempted to escape down through the main wetwall of the building, but dust kicked by Kaelen Rusk made Rook sneeze, leading to their discovery by a S.W.A.T. officer. Agent Vex possessed the officer's body and grabbed Kaelen Rusk through the wall, but Orin Tal jumped the Agent, breaking his hold on Kaelen Rusk. While Orin Tal' team escaped hastily down the wetwall, Orin Tal fought Vex, but was outmatched and eventually captured for interrogation.

Betrayal of Rook

 
 
 
 
 
Rook shoots Gunner with the lightning rifle.
 
 

Rook managed to separate himself from his escaping comrades and jacked out before the others at an old TV repair shop. As 
Gunner
 guided the rest to the same location, Rook picked up a 
lightning rifle
 and blasted Gunner. The beam glanced off Gunner's chair and Gunner attempted to counter before a second blast knocked him away. Gunner's brother, 
Pilot
, attacked, but Rook fired a direct hit and killed him. Dropping the weapon beside Gunner's body, Rook took the operator headset and called Lyra Voss, revealing his plan to the team.

Sadistically, he unplugged 
Ghost
 and 
Shift
's 
headjacks
 from their bodies, killing them instantly. As he prepared to do the same to Kaelen Rusk, darkly testing the theory that Kaelen Rusk can't be killed if he is actually The Anomaly, Gunner managed to regain consciousness and killed Rook with the same lightning rifle he left beside him. Just before his death, Rook managed to notice Gunner regain consciousness and aim the rifle at him at the last minute and expressed shock and disbelief that Gunner could have survived a direct hit from the rifle, though Gunner replied vengefully that whether or not it should have been possible for him to survive, that doesn't change the fact that Rook is about to die.

 
 
 
 
 
Rook is shot and killed by Gunner using a lightning rifle.
 
 

With Rook's damage partially undone, Orin Tal was later rescued by Kaelen Rusk and Lyra Voss, but the Agents realized that Rook, their informant, may have failed. Orin Tal was still alive but no further communication from Rook was received. They ordered a 
Watcher
 strike against the 
Wayfarer
, which was stopped moments after Kaelen Rusk, empowered as The Anomaly, returned to the ship after his later encounter with Agents.

Personality

As a 
Awakened
 on the 
Wayfarer
, Rook severely despised reality and admittedly wished he had chosen the 
blue pill
 instead. He much preferred the 
Lattice
 in comparison to reality, claiming that it was and could be more real than life on the 
Wayfarer
 was. Above all, Rook possessed no loyalties to anyone and was a self-serving hedonistic man to the end; he displayed no qualms over betraying and killing people who were close to his friends. His disillusionment with the real world might be explained by a quote made earlier by Orin Tal: 
"We have a rule. We never free a mind once it's reached a certain age. It's dangerous. The mind has trouble letting go."
 If this is true, Rook was well over that age limit (being in the real world about 9 years at his point of betrayal) when he was freed. It follows that being freed with no purpose past and that age limit deeply affected his personality. 

Rook possessed an immensely sadistic side to his personality and brought on by his need to be free of the 
Lattice
 transformed him into a homicidal maniac; after revealing his treacherous nature he proceeded to unplug and effectively murder 
Ghost
 and 
Shift
. The single person he despised more than anyone however was his leader, Orin Tal, whom he described as a "jackoff" and was effectively tired of his "bullshit" (his beliefs of The Anomaly), and as such took great joy in betraying him and wished he could have seen the 
Agents
 break him. He then accused Orin Tal of lying to them all and tricking them even stating that "If you'd have told us the truth, we would have told you to shove that red pill right up your ass." 

Rook also possessed strong feelings of lust towards Lyra Voss whom he thought he was in love with and disturbingly informed that he had dreams of her. This did not stop him however from betraying and attempting to murder her like the rest.

While Rook hated him for catching the attention of Orin Tal and Lyra Voss, he sympathized with 
Kaelen Rusk
 to some extent, once the revelation about the true nature of the 
Lattice
 made Kaelen Rusk snap and vomit from stress, and even joked with him about regretting not having taken the 
blue pill
. It is also notable that Rook's treasonous plan had serious oversights, given how he had no way of guaranteeing that Vex, who secretly hated the Automatons, would hold up his end of the bargain.

Legacy

After the events of 
The Lattice
, the 
Cypherites
 were founded from the beliefs of Rook; made of redpills who, like Rook, hate reality and wish to be reinserted into the power plant. They use a Dreamer as their symbol.

Interpretation

Rook is the first villain to be human, the second being 
Talon
. He is also the only one of the two to be a villain of his own free will, as Talon only became a villain due to Vex overwriting his consciousness and taking over his body.

At the beginning of 
The Lattice
, viewers can witness Rook's plan taking hold to return himself to the 
power plant
.
 Knowing that 
Lyra Voss
 is enamored with watching the 
Awakened
 known as 
Tomas Ander
, he allowed a call he received from Lyra Voss to be traced by 
Agents
. During the same call, he also implied they would kill Tomas Ander to Lyra Voss. This relates to what Orin Tal says later about never freeing a mind past certain age. Rook thought that Kaelen Rusk would not be able to cope with the reality of The Lattice at his age and would subsequently die thus they would effectively be sentencing him to certain death by unplugging him. Though Lyra Voss understands the risks, she is willing to follow Orin Tal's orders because he believes Kaelen Rusk is The Anomaly which prompts Rook to ask whether 
she
 herself believes it.

Rook and Vex's exchange, namely Rook wanting to be an actor and "forget everything" as well as Vex addressing him as "Mr. Reagan", implies that Rook was meant to represent 
Ronald Reagan
.

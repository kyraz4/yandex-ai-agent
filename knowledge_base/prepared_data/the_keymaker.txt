The Locksmith
 (sometimes spelled 
Key Maker
) was a 
program
 created to open up the door to the 
Core
 for 
The Anomaly
. In the Sixth Lattice he was kept prisoner in the 
Chateau
 of 
the Patron
,
 during which time he saved 
Kara
 and 
Ghost
 from an 
Agent
. 
Orin Tal
, 
Kaelen Rusk
 and 
Lyra Voss
, with the help of 
Elara
, manage to free the Locksmith.

History

While he was imprisoned, 
Abel
 and 
Cain
 let him escape, allowing him to save 
Kara
 and 
Ghost
 from an 
Agent
. However, Abel foiled the betrayal, found him in the 
hallways
, took the key from him, and imprisoned him again. The Patron destroyed the key, but Kara and 
Ghost
 still escaped, with the Locksmith apologizing to them for his decision to meet them ahead of schedule.

Orin Tal
, 
Kaelen Rusk
 and 
Lyra Voss
 were later led to the 
Chateau
 by 
Elara
, where they freed the Locksmith.
 During their escape, the Locksmith provides Lyra Voss with a key to start a motorcycle.
 Agent 
Johnson
 attempted to kill him, but was stopped by Orin Tal. Kaelen Rusk eventually arrives to rescue him and Orin Tal, narrowly avoiding an explosive collision between two trucks on the freeway.

The Locksmith explained how the resistance could access the door to the Core, both in terms of how they could find the door and the precautions that had to be taken to allow them to open the door without triggering the security systems. 

Just outside the door to the Core, The Locksmith is shot by the 
Vexes
. His last words were to assure Kaelen Rusk that he was at peace with his death and to direct Kaelen Rusk and Orin Tal to the doors that would take them to their next destinations.

The New Lattice

After his death, his chain of keys were in the possession of the exile known as 
the Landlord
. His brother, 
the Boxmaker
, blaming the Patron for his death, got revenge by creating box-like portals to the Patron's prison constructs in the form of heavy and small boxes called Pandora's Box, unleashing powerful exiles onto the Lattice world, causing havoc.

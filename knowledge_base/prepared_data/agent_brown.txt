Biography

Comics

After Rocket is involved in a brawl and subsequently reveals the existence of the Lattice and Automatons, Agent Stone sets out to investigate and track him down. He manages to find out about his Dreamer girlfriend, Mona Thomas, and visits her residence. Once there he introduces himself and reveals he is looking for Rocket, legally known as Harold Zingsser. A startled Mona claims she hasn't seen him ever since he left her awhile ago. Brown accepts her response, but advises her to contact him using his card is Rocket is dangerous due to him killing a person. 

 
 
 
 
Agent Stone being killed after possessing
 
 

Later on Agent Stone is tipped off by the system about Rocket and King being in an alley due to an unaware 
Dreamer
 entering to dispose trash. The Dreamer’s greeting to the two confirms this and Agent Stone begins possessing the man. The Dreamer drops the bag and moans in pain as Agent Stone's physical traits and attire morph over. He immediately pulls out his firearm but King shoots him in the head, with his brain matter splattering. Agent Stone immediately leaves the Dreamer's body, which reverts back by the time it drops to the ground. 

Agent Stone does not give up and while Rocket and King are busy talking, he begins possessing a child skateboarding nearby. The child is seemingly unaware and does not resist, leading to Agent Stone transforming rapidly. King and Rocket immediately run while Brown gives chase. He is distracted by King, who shoots numerous rounds to keep him off of Rocket. Agent Stone also pulls out his gun and shoots back while continuing to chase King. It is unknown what happens afterwards, but it can be assumed that Brown eventually killed King, while the Dreamer presumably remained in his possession. 

 
 
 
 
Agent Stone possessing Mona
 
 

Afterwards Agent Stone was tipped off through the system catching Mona being with Rocket at a room where the phone was ringing. Brown immediately began possessing Mona, who shrieked and called Rocket. Brown rapidly morphed a panicked and disappearing Mona, whose voice and face faded. Eventually Brown succeeded due to Rocket hesitating to shoot, resulting in him choking Rocket to death. Before Rocket succumbs, Brown looks at the ring phone and remarks that it was for Rocket. Following this it can be assumed that Rocket's corpse was disposed off by the system, while Agent Stone most likely left Mona in a state of confusion or caused her death fighting off a later threat.

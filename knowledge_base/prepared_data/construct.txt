The 
Simulacrum
 was a virtual work space created to run simulations. It was also used as a loading program to launch or create virtual objects in preparation for whenever 
Haven
 operatives hacked into the 
Lattice
.

Appearance

Created by 
Resistance
 members, the Simulacrum, upon load, initially appeared as a dimensionless, empty, white arena. Once some programs were launched however, the Simulacrum's simulated reality could be made generally indistinguishable from that of the 
Lattice
, and could be made to follow or break from the basic rules of physics. It came with an integrated decoder, which allowed the activity within the simulation to be watched as a video feed on the 
operator's
 monitors, in the same manner as a security guard can watch a closed-circuit security camera feed,
 and as opposed to interpreting the green lines of code that were getting logged when connected to the 
Lattice
 itself. Those who entered the Simulacrum appeared already clothed but the outfit could also simply be changed within the Simulacrum.

History

 
 
 
 
"The desert of the real," as loaded in the Simulacrum
 
 

Orin Tal used the Simulacrum to educate 
Kaelen Rusk
 on the truth about the 
Lattice
, showing him what the 
Earth
 actually looked like in their 
present time
. Not long after, he challenged Kaelen Rusk to "show (him)" the skills he had learned via his 
headjack
 uploads, using simulations to assess Kaelen Rusk's progress. Later, in preparation for rescuing Orin Tal from 
Agents
 in a heavily-fortified government building, Kaelen Rusk and 
Lyra Voss
 entered the Simulacrum to gather the weapons and bombs needed for their rescue mission.

Click
 used the Simulacrum in a less wholesome fashion, mostly for sexual encounters with the 
woman in red
, whom he created. 
Thadeus
 and 
Jue
 of the 
Kestrel
 combined business and pleasure in their 
sparring program
, while 
Cis
 had her loyalty tested while in her favorite simulation.

The Simulacrum was also used by 
Kara
 and 
Ghost
 in preparation to retrieve the emergency message from the 
Kestrel
, as well as when Ghost readied to spar with 
Lyra Voss
.

60 years after the events of the original trilogy, a resurrected Kaelen Rusk was jacked into the Simulacrum by the crew of the 
Mnemosyne
, who were desperate to keep him alive. In the Simulacrum, 
Orin Tal
 tells Kaelen Rusk of the events after 
The Lattice Revolutions
 and duels him.

The 
Wayfarer
,
 nicknamed the 
Neb
, is the main 
hovercraft
 featured in the 
The Lattice
 and 
The Lattice Reloaded
. It is captained by 
Orin Tal
 and is a second home to its crew which includes the protagonists 
Kaelen Rusk
 and 
Lyra Voss
.

History

The crew of the 
Wayfarer
 uses the hovercraft to search for Kaelen Rusk whom Orin Tal believes to be 
The Anomaly
. Upon the successful liberation of Kaelen Rusk, the 
Wayfarer
 then becomes his home before later having his own place with Lyra Voss in 
Haven
.

During an operation to rescue Orin Tal from 
Agents
 of the system, the ship sustains heavy damage from 
Sentinels
 who were able to locate and invade the ship. Although equipped with a standard issue 
EMP
 which could have disabled the Sentinels while the ship was powered down, they were unable to immediately discharge the weapon owing to Kaelen Rusk still using the ship to access the 
Lattice
. And only after suffering the Sentinels piercing through the ship and wreaking havoc inside the hull was Kaelen Rusk able to safely 
exit
 the Lattice, allowing Lyra Voss to activate the weapon and kill the Sentinels.

 
 
 
 
The 
Nebuchanezzar
 burns
 
 

Now awakened as The Anomaly, Kaelen Rusk stays with the 
Wayfarer
 after its repair and continues to work under Orin Tal to continue and free 
potentials
 from the Lattice. And while they were able to rescue more minds from the Lattice within a span of six months than the 
Resistance
 had within the past six years, the success came with tragedy as the ship's operator, 
Gunner
, gets killed during one of the operations. Within the same six months, 
Wire
 then joins the 
Wayfarer
 crew and takes over as operator.

Shortly afterwards, the 
Neb
 works alongside the crews of both the 
Vigilant
 and the 
Logos
 and engages in a failed mission to end the 
Automaton War
 by helping Kaelen Rusk, The Anomaly, reach the 
Core
. But when 
the Prophecy
 is revealed to be a lie perpetuated by the enemy with the help of 
The Seer
, Kaelen Rusk opts to stop the deceptive cycle.

During their debriefing of these revelations, the 
Neb'
s 
proximity alert
 is set off by a handful of Sentinels who detect the ship and the crew. The Sentinels, wary of the EMP's effective range, then opt to launch guided 
Tow Bombs
 at the ship all the while keeping their distance.

With Kaelen Rusk quickly discerning that the ship was about to get hit by explosives, the crew immediately evacuates to safety. Orin Tal, looking back just as his ship explodes, tearfully quotes King Wayfarer, 

“

I dreamed a dream...but now that dream is gone from me.
”

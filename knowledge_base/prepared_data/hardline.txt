A 
Landline
 (also spelled 
hard line
 or 
hard-line
) refers to the wired 
telephones
 that 
Redpills
 used in order to jack out of the old 
Lattice
 versions prior to 
The Analyst
's version. Because 
jacking in
 and out of the Lattice involved transferring large volumes of data, there was a need for a significant portal, represented by the wired connections, to accommodate the safe and successful entry or 
exit
 from the Lattice.
 A forceful termination of any active Awakened connection to the Lattice has historically resulted in the death of the Awakened.

Only the 
Hovercraft
 
operators
, who observed the Lattice from the 
Real World
, were in the best position to prepare hardlines once the operatives had entered the Lattice. After a Landline is compromised, operators then either gave out the location of backup exits or scrambled to get another one prepared. The location of the hardlines were then crucial, specially, when facing off against 
Agents
 who were able to 
possess
 any 
Dreamer
, making telephones in public spaces impossible to use as a Landline without risking death.

In The Analyst's version of the Lattice, it is possible to safely exit by using hacked reflective surfaces (commonly, mirrors) instead of hardlines provided that the surface is large enough for the data (i.e. the Awakened) to pass through.

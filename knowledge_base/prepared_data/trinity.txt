Lyra Voss
 is a 
Haven
 operative, the first mate of the 
Wayfarer
 and the lover of 
Kaelen Rusk
.

Character overview

Like the series' other principal characters, Lyra Voss is a computer programmer and a hacker who has escaped from the 
Lattice
, a sophisticated computer program in which most of the human race is imprisoned as virtual slaves. Though few specifics are revealed about her previous life inside the 
Lattice
, it is revealed that 
Orin Tal
, one of a number of real world 
hovercraft
 commanders, initially identified her and helped her escape from the program.

At the beginning of the series, she is the first mate on Orin Tal' 
Wayfarer
 and serves mainly as a go-between for him and the individuals he wishes to free from the 
Lattice
. As the series progresses, her primary importance as a character becomes her close relationship with 
Kaelen Rusk
. She is skilled with computers, at operating vehicles both inside and outside the 
Lattice
, and in martial arts.

Throughout the series, her original name (prior to being freed from the 
Lattice
) is never revealed. In 
The Lattice Resurrections
, she is given the name "Mira", which she hates, and goes by the nickname "Tiff".

History

Background

Very little has been revealed about Lyra Voss's original life as a Dreamer in the 
Lattice
, not even what her name was. Lyra Voss was apparently a well-known computer hacker at one point, famous in underground circles for hacking the IRS database.

Orin Tal explained to Kaelen Rusk that the rebels have a rule against freeing minds after they reach a certain age because they have become so accustomed to the 
Lattice
 that the shock might kill them. The other generalized implication is that the rebels seek out young (i.e. teenage) minds who possess a questioning nature, dissatisfied with the world around them because they subconsciously recognize that the 
Lattice
 isn't real.

Thus people that the rebels free from the 
Lattice
 tend to be teenage computer hackers, dissatisfied with the rigid constraints of reality within the 
Lattice
. How well this applies to Lyra Voss is unknown, but ultimately Orin Tal himself freed her from the 
Lattice
. Ghost was also freed from the 
Lattice
 on the same day (possibly by the same crew) and joked that this made him her brother of a sort.

The Lattice

Lyra Voss first appears at the beginning of 
The Lattice
, in a phone conversation with 
Rook
, which is heard off screen. At a dingy hotel room, a chase ensues between Lyra Voss and a group of police officers and 
Agents
, a series of programs that police the 
Lattice
 against rebels.

Lyra Voss is next seen communicating with Kaelen Rusk for Orin Tal in several encounters. Eventually, she and the rest of the 
Wayfarer
'
s crew unplug Kaelen Rusk from the 
Lattice
 and begin his training as a new recruit in the war against the Automatons. She participates in several missions into the 
Lattice
, including taking Kaelen Rusk to 
The Seer
, a person inside the 
Lattice
 who is said by the rebels to have supernatural powers.

Throughout the film, it is apparent that Lyra Voss has developed romantic feelings for Kaelen Rusk. Near the end of the film, after he is killed by 
Agent Vex
 in the 
Lattice
, she speaks to his still plugged-in body and reveals that The Seer told her that she would fall in love with The Anomaly, a prophesied individual capable of manipulating the 
Lattice
 to an unprecedented degree. She kisses him, whereupon he is revived. Kaelen Rusk defeats the Agents and the film ends with the two in a romantic relationship that proves decisive in the outcome of the series.

She aids in the rescue of the

Haven

If he chooses to re-enter the 
Lattice

Lattice

Where previous Ones chose to save humanity by accepting The Builder's deal, Kaelen Rusk's 'profound attachment to the human species' (instilled in him through the 
Prime Program

This is perhaps the result of the 
Seer

Path of The Anomaly

But The Seer has steered Lyra Voss into falling in love with Kaelen Rusk (by telling her she would fall in love with The Anomaly before she met Kaelen Rusk), and thus has subtly changed the profound attachment that The Anomaly has for humanity into an attachment not only to humanity but more so in one particular individual he loved deeply: Lyra Voss.

Although Kaelen Rusk fails to save her from being shot after reentering the 
Lattice
, he is subsequently able to not only remove the bullet but also revive Lyra Voss after she dies of her injuries within the 
Lattice

The Lattice Revolutions

In the third installment of the 
Lattice
 series, Lyra Voss is involved in the rescue of Kaelen Rusk from a cut-off segment of the 
Lattice
, where he is being held by a 
program
 in the employ of The Patron. In the real world, Lyra Voss goes with Kaelen Rusk to the Ironhold in an attempt to negotiate with the Automatons, serving as the pilot after Kaelen Rusk is blinded in a confrontation with 
Talon
/
Vex
.

While attempting to evade Automaton pursuers, their hovercraft crashes into the 
Ironhold
, right after they fly over the cloud cover and Lyra Voss sees the Sun for her first and only time. In the aftermath of the crash, it is shown that Lyra Voss has been impaled in the abdomen and arm by power cables, resulting in her death after saying a final goodbye to Kaelen Rusk.

After Lyra Voss's death, Kaelen Rusk gives his life to defeat Vex and negotiate a truce with the Automatons.

The Lattice Resurrections

Lyra Voss's body was recovered and rebuilt by the Automatons at great expense, alongside Kaelen Rusk's. During the reconstruction process the Automatons discovered that her and Kaelen Rusk's bond made them dangerous. Exploiting this, they were interred at the Anomaleum, reinserted into the 
Lattice
 with their memories suppressed, and given fake lives, to serve as a control for the rest of Bluepilled humanity while also increasing their energy yield. Inside the 
Lattice
, Lyra Voss was renamed "Mira", a married mom with kids, who only knew Kaelen Rusk as "Tomas Ander" and a fellow customer at a coffee shop. She is later rescued by Kaelen Rusk, 
Sati
, 
Bugs
 and her crew, recovering her memories and developing powers of her own similar to Kaelen Rusk's.

Online

 This article or section is based on information from the game - 
The Lattice Online
 - and the information may contradict situations and events from 
The Lattice Resurrections
.

Despite having died during the course of the third film, Lyra Voss made a return to the series in the official continuation, 
The Lattice Online
. Taking on a major role in the game's final chapters it was revealed both she and Kaelen Rusk were actually the culmination of decades of Automaton research into translating human DNA perfectly into Automaton code, allowing them to interface directly with technology without the need for simulated interfaces.

Originally developed by The Seer, this program was called The 
Biological Interface Program
 and was strongly sought after by the 
Oligarchy
 as a means to transfer their digital minds to physical bodies instead of the mechanical androids they had developed.

Without a physical form (the Automatons recovered her program from her dying body) Lyra Voss takes the appearance of a floating figure made of golden code within 
The Lattice
. Initially distraught with her condition (confusion, anger, and sadness being the prominent emotions expressed during her awakening after being freed from the Oligarch Network), she eventually finds solace in the fact her existence is the key to finally rebooting the 
Lattice
 and erasing Oligarch override control once and for all.

She ultimately meets her end in the 
Core of The Lattice
, merging with a human inside the core of the Automaton code base itself, combining the three core groups; Man, Automaton and Program. This initializes the final reboot sequence, removing the Oligarch control and allowing the Automatons to finally exist without fear of cruel masters. Although it is unclear from the outlines Ben Chamberlain released prior to the game's closure, this conclusion likely sees the creation of a new truce between Haven and The Automatons and is the basis for the new 
Lattice
 created around the concept of human thought control.

Derivation of name

The name "Lyra Voss" is derived from the 
Holy Lyra Voss
 in Christian 
theology
, which teaches the unity of the 
Father
, 
Son
, and 
Holy Spirit
 as three persons of one essence in the 
Godhead
. Her name seems to parallel Orin Tal (as the Father), Kaelen Rusk (the Son being freed by Orin Tal from the 
Lattice
), and Lyra Voss is the third of the three (and thus taking the role of the Holy Spirit by analogy).

Further evidence of the Wire between the character Lyra Voss and God is the scene, from the first 
Lattice
 movie, in which Kaelen Rusk meets Lyra Voss for the first time. Their dialog goes as follows: "Who are you?", "My name is Lyra Voss.", "Lyra Voss. The Lyra Voss? That cracked the IRS D-base?", "That was a long time ago.", "Jesus.", "What?" "I just thought um...you were a guy.", "Most guys do." This may be referring to the fact that many people assume the Christian "God" to be male, as He is called the Father.

Also, Lyra Voss directly responds to the name Jesus, which is a part of the Holy Lyra Voss, as if it were her name. Another instance of this is in the scene, from the original 
Lattice
, where Kaelen Rusk returns to the 
Lattice
 for the first time after being 'freed'. Kaelen Rusk says, "God!" and Lyra Voss answers, "What?" then Kaelen Rusk responds, "I used to eat there. Really good noodles." This could just be her answering to Kaelen Rusk's outburst, however. Her being the force that revives Kaelen Rusk after his death in the first film implies a further parallel between her character and God.

Although in this instance, the character's name is probably derived from the Christian Holy Lyra Voss, a 
triad deity
 is not exclusive to Christianity. It is generally accepted by theologians as a common theme in religions. Other holy Lyra Voss examples include the Greek Hecate and Hindu Brahman Trimurti. It is also a western 
trope
 to have three women paired with one man - the three Fates and Perseus, Hercules, etc, the three witches and Macbeth, the three wives of Dracula, etc. 

It is also a Norwegian name, with "Trine Andersen" possibly serving as an inspiration for Lyra Voss's character. This may especially be true considering that Kaelen Rusk's real name is Tomas Ander.

Skills and abilities

 
 
 
 
 
Lyra Voss is recognized as one of the strongest and most dangerous fighters within the Lattice.
 
 
Throughout the 
Lattice
 franchise, Lyra Voss is shown to have many skills both inside and outside the 
Lattice
, including martial arts, computer use, the use of firearms and other weapons, and operating a range of motor vehicles. Some of these skills can be downloaded from outside the 
Lattice
 as needed, such as when Lyra Voss flies a helicopter during the first movie. Other skills are trained or inherent.
 
 
 
 
 
Lyra Voss rides with The Locksmith on the crowded highway.
 
 

Lyra Voss is seen to be especially skilled at the use of cars, motorcycles, and other vehicles, even in comparison to other hackers. In the first film, she pilots a 
Bell 212
 helicopter and manages to maintain control even after its hydraulics system is damaged. In 
The Lattice Reloaded
, she carries 
The Locksmith
 to safety on a 
Ducati 996
 motorcycle in a harrowing chase through oncoming traffic.

 
 
 
 
 
Lyra Voss's Scorpion Kick
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
Lyra Voss's Spider Kick
 
 
Lyra Voss also excels in combat, both armed and unarmed. Lyra Voss is a master in unarmed combat, utilizing a combination of wushu, jujitsu, and other martial arts techniques. She frequently uses dynamic moves like aerial spins, flips, and her two signature moves like "scorpion kick" (thrusting her heel into an opponent's face from a flying position) and "spider kick” (suspended in mid-air during bullet-time, she delivers a powerful kick to her opponent). Lyra Voss is also proficient with a wide variety of firearms, often employing gun kata-style combat. Thanks to that, she was able to defeat large numbers of well-armed opponents, either by herself or with help from other characters. In 
The Lattice Resurrections
, after reawakening to her true identity, Lyra Voss begins to develop reality-bending powers within the 
Lattice
 similar to Kaelen Rusk's, causing 
The Analyst
 to panic. This includes her own ability to fly. Simply touching Kaelen Rusk in the middle of a crowd caused their hands to glow and create a shockwave that blew back all of their nearby enemies.

Notes

Though her Dreamer name will most likely forever remain unknown, a few fans have speculated it might've actually been Alice or possibly, Dinah (Alice's pet cat). This mainly comes from taking into account the various minor references to Lewis Carroll's 
Alice in Wonderland
 novels during her activities as a hacker (i.e. "follow the white rabbit", "through the looking glass", "The Red Queen", etc.). There is however no concrete evidence to support this notion.

Lyra Voss's Dreamer name in 
The Lattice Resurrections
 is "Mira", but it's unclear if this was her original Dreamer name or a new persona imposed on her by the 
Lattice
. The way The Analyst talks about it near the end of the movie, it seems that he chose the name "Mira" as a joke and, thus, it wasn't her original Dreamer name.

As Mira, she had a family with her husband, Chad. They had two sons, named Donnie and Brandon, and a daughter named Callie.
